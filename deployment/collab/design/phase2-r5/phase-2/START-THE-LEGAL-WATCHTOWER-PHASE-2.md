## INSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE

THE LEGAL WATCHTOWER

single opaque agent, or collection of disconnected tools.

It is a persistent, institutionally organized, metacognitive legal-intelligence
entity for continuous observation and reconstruction of the Greek legal order,
including:

- legislation and amendments;
- Government Gazette material and promulgation evidence;
- codifications and temporal legal validity;

document length, or prestige analogies. It is satisfied only by inspectable

   transitions, invalid transitions, invariants, temporal semantics, failure

   contenders from primary sources.
5. It uses Common Lisp cosmetically rather than because specific Lisp semantics
   materially strengthen the institutional model.
6. It creates duplicate truth, duplicate authority, hidden mutable ownership,
   silent degradation, or a wrapper around an unresolved capability.
7. It optimizes for simplicity, short delivery, implementation convenience,
   model context, cost, or conventional industry practice.
8. It describes metacognition as logging, telemetry, prompt reflection, or a
   dashboard rather than an operational self-model with causal effect.

## COMMON LISP REQUIREMENT

The architecture must exploit Common Lisp natively wherever this creates a
material semantic, introspective, correctness, recovery, performance, or
evolvability advantage.

Evaluate, rather than mechanically mandate:

- functions, closures, lexical ownership, multiple values, and destructuring;
- CLOS generic protocols and multi-method dispatch;
- method combinations for institutionally governed cross-cutting behavior;
- class redefinition and controlled live evolution;
- MOP facilities where true meta-level control is required;
- macros only for genuine domain languages or compile-time guarantees;
- conditions and restarts separating failure detection from recovery policy;
- dynamic bindings only for explicit institutional context;
- packages and ASDF systems as authority and dependency boundaries;
- precise representations, specialized arrays, compiler facilities, and
  implementation-specific mechanisms where evidence establishes an advantage;
- formal specification, model checking, theorem proving, and proof-producing
  boundaries where critical invariants justify them.

Do not imitate these mechanisms manually through string dispatch, generic maps,
central conditional registries, exception-only control, passive record classes,
or service-container ceremony.

Select ANSI portability or implementation-specific power by evidence, not dogma.
