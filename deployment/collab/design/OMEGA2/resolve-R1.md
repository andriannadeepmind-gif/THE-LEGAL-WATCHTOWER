## 1. What R-1 actually asserts — and the false dichotomy inside it

R-1 as stated is a disjunction with two branches, both declared losing:

- **Branch (a):** the system emits a *sub-certified trusted answer* under deadline → violates INV
  (unjustified trusted claim). TRUE — this branch genuinely cannot be taken. It stays forbidden here.
- **Branch (b):** the system emits *honest UNKNOWN* → at a no-next-round node this "= conceding." This is
  where the canon's framing smuggles in a **conflation**, and the whole resolution turns on separating it.

The conflation: R-1 treats these two propositions as the same event —
- **P₁ (system):** *the system produces a machine-checked certificate for the novel move at speech latency.*
- **P₂ (client):** *the client takes the best available lawful action at the node.*

R-1 correctly proves **P₁ is impossible** for a genuinely novel move at true speech latency — that
impossibility is real, irreducible, and shared by every human on earth (no one certifies a first-impression
legal question in ninety seconds). But R-1 then asserts **¬P₁ ⇒ ¬P₂** ("UNKNOWN = concede"), which is
**false**. The step assumes the only thing standing between the client and concession is a *system
emission*. It is not. Between UNKNOWN-from-the-system and concession sits the entity that has held decision
authority the whole time: **the human advocate** (HA-1, "the lawyer speaks"). There is a **third branch**
R-1's disjunction omits:

- **Branch (c):** the system emits **nothing through the trust gate**; it hands the advocate **stamped,
  uncertified decision-support** with a complete manifest of what it did and did not check; **the human
  bears the call** in their own professional capacity. This is neither a trusted emission (so INV is
  untouched) nor a concession (the advocate acts).

Branch (c) is not available for *free* — it does not give the human a *certificate*. But it changes what
must be shown from "the system safely certifies novelty at speed" (impossible, keep it impossible) to "the
client, at the node, is no worse off than with an elite unaided human, and the system's invariant is
intact" (achievable, §5). The rest of this document builds branch (c) rigorously, quantifies how large the
*genuinely* novel-at-zero-recess residue really is once (a) enumeration and (b) time-manufacture have run,
and states the honest final label.

## 2. (a) Pre-computation / preparation dominance — how much "novelty" it removes, and why a residue remains

**Mechanism.** Before the node, exhaustively pre-certify the *reachable move product* for the matter:
`Q × I × M` = {characterization (which legal category / Qualifikation)} × {interpretation (how each
candidate rule reads, incl. defeat-semantics variants)} × {opponent move / objection / counter-authority}.
Cost, compute, and staffing are **not constraints** (binding project condition), so the enumeration budget
is bounded only by the *finiteness of the reachable product for this matter's actual facts and the
applicable corpus* — not by resources. Each enumerated tuple gets a pre-certified response and a
**speech-latency-indexed fallback** (retrieval, not fresh proof). At the node, an opponent move that lies
inside the enumerated product is a **lookup**, not a certification: it is only *novel-to-an-unprepared-mind*,
not *novel-in-the-space*.

**Fraction of "novelty" removed (honest decomposition, not a fabricated percentage).** Apparent novelty at
a hearing has three disjoint sources; enumeration disposes of them unequally:

| Source of apparent novelty | Removed by exhaustive prep? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Novel-to-this-advocate but inside `Q×I×M`** (the human simply didn't hold it in memory) | **YES — fully** | it was pre-certified; at the node it is a lookup |
| **Facts not yet in the record** (surprise document, ambush; LA-5 weak Greek discovery) | **NO** | you cannot pre-certify a response to a fact you have not seen |
| **A legal characterization/theory no available generator produced** (genuine first-impression + R-3 population blind spot) | **NO** | you cannot enumerate an idea the entire model+human population available to you does not generate |
| **Law that changed between prep and hearing** | mostly YES (re-prep on change, F12/`firm-operations`) | rare at hearing latency; the versioned rule base re-certifies |

**Result.** Because the enumeration budget is unbounded by fiat and the reachable product for a *specific
matter* is finite, prep removes the **entire first row and the last row** — which is, in practice, the
*large majority* of what unprepared litigators experience as "surprise." It provably **cannot** remove rows
2 and 3. So (a) does not shrink the node to nothing; it **collapses "novelty" to its two irreducible cores**:
(i) **concealed/surprise facts** (LA-5) and (ii) **genuinely-novel legal ideas** (R-3 population blind spot
∩ true first impression). This is a **reduction of the novelty set to `LA-5 ∪ R-3`**, tagged
`[DESIGN-ENTAILED that these two survive; the removed fraction is the finite complement, unbounded-budget]`.
It is *not* elimination of the node — stating otherwise would be the circular failure.

## 3. (b) One-shot detection + buy-time: converting a one-shot into a two-shot under real Greek procedure

**The "zero-recess" premise is itself partly over-claimed.** R-1 says the one-shot node has "no next round."
Real Greek interim/urgent procedure supplies **lawful time-manufacture devices** that frequently convert a
*zero*-recess node into a *short*-recess node — hours to days — which is **enough for the ordinary
certification pipeline to run** (the pipeline `§7` said "cannot run live" runs perfectly well in a
two-day σημείωμα window; it was never required to run at *speech* latency, only the node's *decision* was).

**The devices (EMPIRICAL-legal, versioned-rule-based, human-owned characterization):**

- **Αίτημα προθεσμίας για σημείωμα / υπόμνημα** in ασφαλιστικά (ΚΠολΔ 682 seq.). Greek interim judges
  **routinely** grant a short post-hearing deadline to file written σημειώματα. This is the single most
  reliable converter: the oral one-shot becomes an oral-then-written two-step, and the full pipeline runs
  in the written window.
- **Αναβολή** of the ασφαλιστικά συζήτηση (ΚΠολΔ 241, discretionary).
- **Προσθήκη–αντίκρουση** where the track provides it (ordinary track ΚΠολΔ 237, 15-day window) — not an
  interim device but the same converter for merits nodes mislabeled one-shot.
- **Opposing the immediacy of a προσωρινή διαταγή** (ΚΠολΔ 691Α, as amended by Ν. 4335/2015): the
  respondent is summoned to the ΠΔ hearing and may argue against issuance, for a narrower order, or for a
  short deferral; and if the ασφαλιστικά hearing is adjourned, the fate of the ΠΔ is re-decided (691Α §2) —
  a second bite the naive "one-shot" reading misses.
- **Αίτημα μη έκδοσης / αναστολής έκδοσης απόφασης** — request the court defer its ruling.

**When it WORKS:** absent genuine κατεπείγον, Greek courts grant σημείωμα deadlines and short αναβολές as a
matter of ordinary practice; the respondent to a ΠΔ can usually obtain a brief hearing rather than an
instant ex parte order. In these (empirically common) postures the node is **DISSOLVED into a two-shot** and
R-1's "no next round" premise is simply **false for that node**.

**When it FAILS (court refuses — state exactly):** (1) genuine **κατεπείγον** where imminent irreversible
harm forces an immediate ΠΔ; (2) a **hard on-the-spot preclusion** — the *object-now-or-waive* objection
during oral argument, a limitation/appeal term expiring at that instant — where the "act" *is* the deadline
and no motion can move it; (3) the judge **discretionarily decides on the spot** and declines σημειώματα;
(4) the opponent **holds** the ΠΔ and the court, weighing immediacy, refuses your delay. At these instants
the recess window is **genuinely zero** and (b) yields nothing.

**Critical honesty caveat — buy-time is NOT universally good (ties to `firm-operations` §8 + D4).** Delay
**cuts both ways**: if your client *holds* a favorable ΠΔ, or is the party racing the clock (the D4
"client bankrupt in six months" objective), *buying time harms the client*. The time-manufacture planner is
therefore **subordinate to the recorded client-objective / time-to-relief model** — it *surfaces* the
recess option and its trade-off; it **never auto-adjourns**. A naive "always seek recess" organ would
reintroduce F9 (won-the-motion-lost-the-client) and negate D4. `[DESIGN-ENTAILED]`

**Result.** (b) **DISSOLVES** the sub-class of nodes that are one-shot only by under-preparation of
procedure — a large, empirically dominant fraction — leaving a residue of **genuinely court-refused,
truly-zero-recess instants** (the four failure cases above). Tag: `[DESIGN-ENTAILED the converter exists;
EMPIRICAL which fraction of real nodes the court will convert; HYPOTHESIS per case]`.

## 4. (c) A fast decision-support tier whose output is NEVER a trusted emission — why it does NOT re-open branch (a)

**The precise structural claim.** R-1 branch (a) fails because a *sub-certified tier that emits* re-admits
"unjustified trusted claim under deadline." The forbidden class is defined **entirely by what crosses trust
gate K**. Therefore build the fast tier so that **its output never crosses K**:

- **It is an R3 (untrusted) organ**, off the trusted path by construction — the same class the canon already
  concedes is *absorbable* (`beat-the-canon` Part 4 classifies the one-shot detector and the reversibility
  ledger as absorbable; this extends them by *one artifact type*, not by a new trust tier).
- **Its every artifact is stamped `UNCERTIFIED-SUPPORT`** and carries the **unchecked-dependency manifest**
  (the premise-trust ledger, `autopsy` Claim 1): "reasoned X; did **not** verify {authority pinpoint,
  defeat-semantics, characterization Q, exchangeability}; assurance = anytime-bound, not certified."
- **It is addressed to the human advocate, never to the forum.** The system emits *nothing* to the court.
- **The advocate's on-the-spot assertion is a human act** under δικηγορική ευθύνη — exactly what an unaided
  elite lawyer does, and exactly where HA-1 already places authority ("the lawyer speaks").

**Does this re-introduce the forbidden class? No — the invariant test passes term by term.** INV forbids the
*system* emitting an *unjustified trusted claim*. Here: (i) there is **no trusted claim** — nothing passes K;
(ii) there is **no unjustified claim by the system** — the artifact is *maximally* justified in the honest
sense: it states precisely what it checked and what it did not (four-valued honesty *strengthened*, not
weakened); (iii) the decision is a **human authority point**, not a machine seal (A4/BO-33 heteronomy
intact). Branch (a) admitted a *weaker gate*; branch (c) adds **no gate at all** — it keeps K exactly as
strict and routes the speed-sensitive work *outside* it. The "unjustified trusted claim under deadline"
class is therefore **not re-opened; it is made structurally unreachable** (there is no sub-certified
*emission* to be unjustified).

**The one honest residual this introduces — automation bias (D10).** A flustered human under deadline may
treat `UNCERTIFIED-SUPPORT` as if certified. This is real and is **CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED, not eliminated**:
the stamp + dependency manifest + the semantic-delta adjudication budget (tournament D10) + advocate
training reduce it; the residual is socio-technical, EMPIRICAL, never zero. It is *smaller* than the status
quo (an unaided human under the same pressure has *no* manifest telling them what is unverified), so the
tier is a net reduction on this axis too — but the floor is disclosed, not claimed away.

## 5. (d) Is the truly-novel-at-zero-time residue SMALLER than what an elite human faces?

Partition the residue by axis and answer each honestly:

| Axis of the zero-recess node | System vs elite unaided human | Label |
|---|---|---|
| **Enumeration completeness** (is this move inside `Q×I×M`?) | System pre-holds vastly more of the reachable product than any human memory → many moves "novel" to the human are **lookups** for the system | **strictly SMALLER** residue (ELIMINATED sub-class) |
| **Retrieval at speed** (get the pre-certified fallback / pinpoint authority now) | Instant indexed lookup vs human recall under stress | **strictly SMALLER** |
| **One-shot-node detection** (is this preclusive? is a recess obtainable?) | System flags the zero-recess node + names the exact time-manufacture motion the flustered human forgets | **strictly SMALLER** |
| **Speed of analyzing a *within-space* novel implication** | Anytime-bounded fast tier hands structured analysis faster than a human derives it | **SMALLER** |
| **Genuinely-novel legal *idea*** (R-3 population blind spot ∩ true first impression) | If no available generator produced it, neither system nor the human population reliably has it | **EQUAL at best** (REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT) |
| **Concealed-fact ambush** (LA-5 weak discovery) | Both meet the surprise at the instant; system reacts faster but neither pre-holds it | **EQUAL** |
| **Final *certified* correctness of the novel call** | **Impossible for both** at true speech latency | **EQUAL — irreducible (P₁ is impossible for everyone)** |

**Net (d):** the residue is made **strictly smaller than the elite unaided human's on every
enumeration / retrieval / detection / speed axis** (real, DESIGN-ENTAILED gains — the system *shrinks the
human's novelty set and improves their inputs*), and is **equal to the elite human's on the pure-novel-idea,
concealed-fact, and certified-correctness axes** — which is the definition of REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT. It is
therefore **≤ the elite human's residue everywhere and strictly < on several axes**, but **not zero** and
**not smaller on the deepest axis**. Claiming smaller-on-the-deepest-axis would be dishonest: a
first-impression dispositive legal judgment at true zero-recess has **no certificate available to any
party**, and the system's honesty forbids it from manufacturing one.

## 6. THE MECHANISM — the Zero-Recess Protocol (ZRP), three layers over one node

1. **Enumeration-dominance layer (pre-node).** Exhaustive pre-certification of `Q×I×M` for the matter +
   speech-latency-indexed fallback library. Turns novelty-by-under-preparation into lookup (§2).
2. **Time-manufacture layer (at-node), objective-conditioned.** One-shot-node detector (reversibility +
   "next round exists?" + **"recess obtainable?"**) paired with a legitimate-recess planner that surfaces
   the exact Greek device (σημείωμα-προθεσμία, αναβολή, 691Α opposition, προσθήκη, μη-έκδοση) **with its
   procedural basis** — *and its trade-off against the recorded client-objective / time-to-relief model*
   (§3). On a granted recess, the **existing trusted K-gated pipeline runs in the window** (no new tier).
3. **Uncertified fast-support layer (true-zero-recess residue).** When time cannot be bought and the move is
   genuinely novel: emit **nothing** through K; hand the advocate a `UNCERTIFIED-SUPPORT` artifact
   (anytime-bounded best analysis + unchecked-dependency manifest + assurance level). The human bears the
   call (§4). Honest UNKNOWN is still emitted *as* UNKNOWN, but *accompanied* by uncertified support, so it
   is no longer *operationally* a concession — the advocate has material to act on.

### 5-tuple

- **Seat.** R3 untrusted organ (ZRP planner + fast-support tier) over the Greek-procedure state machine,
  entirely off the trusted path; the certification it triggers on a granted recess is the *unchanged*
  trusted path. **No new trust tier is created.**
- **Interface.** classify node → `{reversible?, next-round-exists?, recess-obtainable?}`. If recess
  obtainable ∧ recess favors the client-objective → surface the specific motion + run full certification in
  the window. Else if move ∈ enumerated `Q×I×M` → speech-latency fallback lookup. Else (genuinely novel ∧
  true-zero-recess) → emit `UNCERTIFIED-SUPPORT` (assurance + dependency manifest), addressed to the human,
  **never to gate K, never to the forum.**
- **Invariant.** Nothing crosses K without full certification (INV intact — the strict gate is untouched).
  Every fast-support artifact is stamped UNCERTIFIED with its dependency manifest and is human-addressed
  (the system emits no untrusted trusted-claim — the forbidden class is *unreachable*, not merely
  forbidden). The one-shot decision is a human-authority point (HA-1). The recess planner is subordinate to
  the recorded client-objective (never auto-adjourns; F9/D4 preserved).
- **Failure.** The genuinely-novel dispositive move at a **court-refused** true-zero-recess instant: the
  human decides on uncertified support alone (bounded below by `R-3 ∪ LA-5`). Plus the automation-bias
  sub-residual (D10): the human mistakes UNCERTIFIED for certified — contained, not eliminated.
- **Verification.** Seed one-shot scenarios (ασφαλιστικά with σημείωμα refused; on-the-spot preclusive
  objection; immediate 691Α ΠΔ held by the opponent). Assert: (i) detection + escalation, **not** silent
  queue-for-next-round; (ii) recess planner surfaces the correct motion where procedurally available **and**
  flags the client-objective trade-off; (iii) fast-support artifacts **never carry a trusted stamp and
  never pass K** (fail the run if any does); (iv) **measure the residue** = fraction of seeded nodes that
  are simultaneously *genuinely-novel* ∧ *recess-refused* ∧ *outside the enumerated fallback*. **This
  fraction must be reported non-zero** — a run reporting zero residue is flagged as suspected
  circular-resolution (the honesty check on this very document).

## 7. Why this is not a patch, a wrapper, or a relabel (creator-law compliance)

- **Not a patch / guard-around-a-bad-shape:** it does not weaken K to let novelty through (that would be
  branch (a)). It removes the *demand* that K certify novelty at speed, by separating the system-emission
  event from the human-decision event that always owned the node. The error class ("system emits
  unjustified trusted claim under deadline") is made **structurally unreachable** — there is no
  sub-certified emission to *be* unjustified — which is the creator law "εξάλειψη της κλάσης σφάλματος"
  over "φρουρός γύρω από λάθος σχήμα."
- **Not a wrapper / second head:** one gate (K) for all trusted emission; the fast tier is a *different
  concept* (uncertified human decision-support), one seat, not a duplicate of K.
- **Not honest-ignorance abandoned:** the fast tier is *more* honest, not less — it emits its full
  unchecked-dependency manifest; τίμια άγνοια is preserved and *augmented* with actionable support.

## 8. Honesty hunt — the two forbidden failures, checked

- **Circular ("solved by redefining the problem")?** No. P₁ (system certifies novelty at speed) is left
  **exactly as impossible** as R-1 proved it — not redefined, not weakened. What is separated is P₁ from P₂
  (client gets best lawful action), which R-1 illicitly welded together via "UNKNOWN = concede." Separating
  a genuine conflation is DISSOLUTION, not redefinition. The residue is *reported non-zero by construction*
  (§6 verification iv) precisely to bar the circular claim.
- **Relocated to R-2 (the honesty tax)?** No — and this is the sharpest self-check. The fast tier hands the
  human **honest uncertified analysis**; it does **not** instruct the human to assert-at-full-confidence.
  *Whether* the advocate then bluffs on that support is the R-2 question, and it is left **exactly where it
  was** — with the human, EMPIRICAL/UNKNOWN, untouched by this resolution. R-1 asks whether the *system* can
  support the node without violating INV and without forcing concession; that is what is resolved-to-limit
  here. R-2 asks whether the *human* should exceed what they can prove; not addressed, not smuggled. The
  two stay cleanly separated. (Verification: the fast-support artifact's schema contains **no
  confidence-inflation field**; it carries only honest analysis + the unchecked-dependency manifest.)
- **Relocated to R-3?** Partly *inherited*, not relocated: the irreducible residue is explicitly the
  intersection with R-3 (population blind spot) and LA-5 (concealed fact). We name it as inherited from
  already-BLOCKING residuals, not as newly created here.
