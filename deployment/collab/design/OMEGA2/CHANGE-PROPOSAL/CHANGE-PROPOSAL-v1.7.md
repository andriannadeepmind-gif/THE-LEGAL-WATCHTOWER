continuously-evolving, proof-carrying legal institution and machine trust/citation layer — not a document base,
search engine, LLM wrapper, state mirror, mere knowledge graph, chatbot or API. **The act of enacting a law or
decision belongs to its real issuer; LAWMAX certifies identity/provenance/temporality/coding/completeness/
relations/interpretation/machine-trust** (`:RA-I-1`). The public profile is the first full implementation; the
private lawyer profile is added later with no fundamental teardown and no public dependency (§15, v1.6 §5).
