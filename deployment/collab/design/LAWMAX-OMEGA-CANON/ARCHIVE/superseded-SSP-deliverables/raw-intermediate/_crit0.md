Verdict tags: **NOT-A-CEILING** (feasible mechanism today exceeds the stated maximum), **KIND-INFLATED** (ABSOLUTE claimed, actually MODEL_RELATIVE/RESOURCE_BOUNDED), **CIRCULAR** (derivation assumes the mechanism it then "attains").

## P0 — ceiling falsified or derivation circular

**P0-1. AX-01 — NOT-A-CEILING + CIRCULAR. The "unique maximal semantics" claim is false; the ceiling is shaped to fit the incumbent JTMS seat.**

Quoted (derivation): *"the grounded/well-founded semantics is the unique maximal semantics that is simultaneously deterministic (single minimal complete extension), polynomial-time computable … Any 'stronger' semantics either loses uniqueness (breaks H5 replay) or decidability (breaks H3 fail-closed termination)."*

Attack: the derivation attacks only **credulous** acceptance ("credulous acceptance under preferred/stable … is NP-hard and non-unique") and silently generalizes to all stronger semantics. False. **Sceptical** acceptance under preferred semantics, and **ideal**/eager semantics, are (i) unique per query (deterministic — H5 holds), (ii) decidable on the finite committed rulebase (H3 termination holds), (iii) certificate-carrying with finite offline-checkable certificates. What is lost is only **polynomial time** — and polynomiality is not an H1–H8 invariant anywhere in the freeze; AX-17's own machinery (certified cost budgets) exists precisely to govern cost. Ideal-semantics acceptance strictly contains grounded acceptance, so a strictly stronger, H1–H8-conforming semantics is feasible today. The circularity: the attainment_mechanism is *"Extend the existing JTMS engine … into a certified alternating-fixpoint labeller"* — the ceiling is exactly what the incumbent seat already computes, and the false uniqueness lemma is the only thing making it a ceiling.

Correction: either (a) restate the ceiling as *"grounded extension is the ceiling **within polynomial-time-certifiable, per-query-unique semantics**"* and change `ceiling_kind` on this leg to RESOURCE_BOUNDED (the bound is compute cost, not possibility); or (b) raise the ceiling to sceptical-preferred/ideal acceptance with certified exhaustive-extension certificates under an AX-17 cost budget, and prove grounded ⊊ ideal as the recorded gap. Under the supreme law, option (b) must at minimum be **named** as the superior conception.

**P0-2. AX-06 / AX-13 / AX-18 — CIRCULAR + KIND-INFLATED. "Recompute-and-compare is the supremum" is derived from H8, which is *defined as* recompute-and-compare; the dismissal of succinct proofs rests on a false cryptographic premise.**

Quoted (AX-13 derivation): *"Mode (b) cannot exceed (a) in assurance — it adds trust in the proof system's soundness and in a correct arithmetization of the full semantics, and arithmetizing an SBCL pipeline is infeasible today — so recompute-and-compare over committed inputs is the ceiling, which is precisely H8's form."*
Quoted (AX-18 derivation): *"sublinear verification requires succinct proof systems (SNARKs) whose trusted setup and toolchain fall outside 'feasible today with standard cryptography'."*
Quoted (AX-06 derivation): *"Recompute-and-compare is therefore both necessary and sufficient — the same argument that makes AX-13's 'offline verifiability cannot exceed recompute-and-compare' absolute."* and *"verifying n artifacts with s total steps requires Ω(s) recomputation."*

Attack, three prongs. (1) **Circularity**: H8 is the axiom "recompute-and-compare, never a declared root." Deriving "recompute-and-compare is the ceiling" from H8 proves nothing about the world — it restates the axiom. If H8 means *zero producer trust* (its stated intent), then transparent succinct proofs satisfy it and the Ω(s)/Ω(k) cost floors collapse. (2) **False premise**: STARK-class transparent proof systems have **no trusted setup** — their assumptions are hash collision-resistance, the *same assumption family* the ceilings already accept for SHA-256 commitments. The corpus's own frontier concedes this: *"AX-13/AX-06: a ceiling assuming audit/validation cost grows with history length is exceedable — proof-carrying CRDTs validate an update and its full causal history in constant time and space via succinct proofs"* (PaPoC 2025, an implemented system). (3) **Resource language inside ABSOLUTE labels**: "infeasible today" and "feasible today with standard cryptography" are, by the corpus's own taxonomy (see AX-21's RESOURCE_BOUNDED rationale), resource-boundedness — an ABSOLUTE ceiling may not lean on toolchain availability.

Correction: split each ceiling into (a) an ABSOLUTE **assurance-semantics** clause — "the verifier's knowledge is bounded by what is derivable from committed bytes under stated cryptographic assumptions; recompute-and-compare and transparent proof-of-recomputation are equally maximal under the same assumption family" — and (b) a RESOURCE_BOUNDED **cost** clause — "linear-cost recompute is the operative floor given the declared SBCL toolchain (no arithmetization exists for it); a transparent proof toolchain would strictly lower verification cost with no assurance loss." Delete "necessary" from AX-06's "necessary and sufficient" and delete the two-history Ω(s) floor as absolute. Relabel AX-13 and AX-06 accordingly or scope their ABSOLUTE claims to the assurance clause only.

**P0-3. AX-09 + unknown_rule — NOT-A-CEILING, and internally contradicted by the corpus's own closure.**

Quoted (AX-09 ceiling): *"(d) UNKNOWN is a terminal object: no operation maps it to pass/equality (H2)."*
Quoted (unknown_rule): *"(4) UNKNOWN propagates — any derived fact computed over an UNKNOWN input (including the global dominance conjunction) is itself UNKNOWN, never defaulted."*

Attack: **unconditional** propagation is strictly below the attainable maximum. Certified invariance discharge — three-valued Kleene evaluation, exhaustive case-split, and exactly the van Woerkom stability computation the corpus itself cites — soundly derives a KNOWN verdict over UNKNOWN inputs whenever the verdict is invariant over **all completions** of the unknown (trivially: `A ∨ ⊤ = ⊤` for unknown A; non-trivially: a focus case "stable" over all dimension completions). This is not guessing (H2 intact: the certificate quantifies over completions and is offline-recheckable), deterministic (H5), and fail-closed (no invariance certificate → UNKNOWN stands). The corpus convicts itself twice: the frontier ceiling_threat says *"a ceiling treating blind-spot analysis as necessarily heuristic/enumerative is exceedable by exact stability computation,"* and closure DIM-12 **absorbs** `exact-stability-computation` into K-SUP — a family whose output is exactly a KNOWN fact derived over an UNKNOWN input, which unknown_rule(4) forbids. The freeze and the ceiling-envelope synthesis are inconsistent.

Correction: amend clause (d) and unknown_rule(4) to: *"UNKNOWN propagates to every derived fact **except** one carrying a certified invariance (stability) proof that its value is identical under all completions of the unknown input; the input remains UNKNOWN, the derived verdict is KNOWN-with-stability-certificate; absence of such a certificate leaves the derived fact UNKNOWN."* The AX-09 ceiling statement gains a fourth branch: PASS/FAIL-stable-over-unknowns as a certified verdict class. Without this fix, unknown_rule(4) also poisons the tournament itself: a candidate with one UNKNOWN axis blocks verdicts even on pairs whose outcome is invariant to that axis's value.

## P1 — kind inflated or a clause exceedable by a feasible mechanism

**P1-1. AX-03 — NOT-A-CEILING on the arbitration clause; contradicted by the corpus's own K-BFT class.**

Quoted: *"all inter-agent arbitration is reduced to deterministic, data-defined admission at one kernel-enforced writer seat — never to timing, races, or votes"*; derivation: *"enforced below the code by kernel DAC (EACCES), which is the strongest arbitration available on a single-authority operating model."*

Attack: (a) The single-authority qualifier lives only in the derivation, not the ceiling_statement — and the closure's own K-BFT is declared *"REGION: U_T (H4 satisfied at the logical-sequence level; H5 by deterministic consensus replay)"*: a BFT-total-ordered single **logical** writer is H1–H8-admissible and strictly stronger arbitration (operator-independent) than one OS-DAC seat. The same document cannot hold both. (b) The FLP invocation is misapplied — FLP bounds deterministic *asynchronous* consensus; partially-synchronous BFT (Mysticeti, in production) is unaffected. (c) The confluence boundary — *"no feasible system can admit more concurrency than the commuting fraction of its effect algebra"* — is false: deterministic ordered conflict resolution (Calvin-class deterministic concurrency control: pre-committed serialization order, concurrent execution of **non-commuting** transactions with deterministic outcome) yields output that is a pure function of frozen inputs while exceeding the antichain/disjoint-footprint concurrency fraction. Correction: restate the ceiling as "single logical serialization point (physical single-writer OR machine-checked-nonforking replicated total order), with concurrency bounded by deterministic conflict-ordered execution, not by effect commutativity"; strike the FLP sentence; keep RESOURCE_BOUNDED.

**P1-2. AX-07 / AX-12 (and AX-08's "(b) ABSOLUTE" note) — KIND-INFLATED: fork-consistency caps conditioned on a resource that exists today.**

Quoted (AX-07): *"No feasible system can exceed (c) without a live external witness quorum"* — `ceiling_kind: ABSOLUTE`. Quoted (AX-12 derivation): *"full split-view resistance requires an external cosigning quorum, which is not within unilateral reach today."*

Attack: by the corpus's own labelling logic, AX-21 is RESOURCE_BOUNDED *because* "a verified compiler would strictly shrink the TCB, and B0's declared resources exclude it." Identically: a witness quorum would strictly lift the fork-consistency cap, and B0's declared resources exclude it (witness-policy.sexp NIL). Parity forces RESOURCE_BOUNDED. Worse, the "not within unilateral reach" premise is stale against the corpus's own findings: the transparency.dev witness network is operating cross-ecosystem with *"a shared community-vetted log configuration removing per-log onboarding friction"* and hardware witnesses — enrolling is a feasible act today, not an unreachable resource. Secondary: AX-07's *"a process cannot produce a producer-trust-free proof of its own current runtime state"* is weakened by TEE remote attestation, which shifts trust to a hardware-vendor anchor — the same category of irreducible anchor AX-12's own derivation accepts for genesis keys; the clause should say "producer-trust-free without introducing a hardware trust anchor," not "cannot." Correction: relabel AX-07 and AX-12 RESOURCE_BOUNDED (or carve the witness-conditioned clauses out as RESOURCE_BOUNDED sub-bounds, as AX-08 tried to — and AX-08's "(b) ABSOLUTE" must carry the same carve-out).

**P1-3. AX-14 — exceedable clause: "cannot determinize the untrusted producers" is now false.**

Quoted: *"Two things NO feasible system can do, and the ceiling honestly excludes: determinizing the untrusted producers themselves (H1 confines them instead)…"* and *"the ONLY nondeterminism anywhere in the system lives outside the trusted path (LLM candidates, network fetches)."*

Attack: the corpus's own frontier threat states it — *"batch-invariant kernels … deliver bit-identical outputs under frozen inputs at ~34% throughput overhead, i.e. substrate-level determinism is achievable."* With OMS-pinned weights + batch-invariant inference, candidate generation becomes a pure function of the committed closure (per hardware/driver class). The attainable maximum is therefore strictly higher than stated: full-system replay **including** the untrusted proposal layer, with the residue being cross-hardware portability. Correction: replace the exclusion with *"determinizing untrusted producers is attainable per pinned hardware/software class (pinned weights + batch-invariant kernels) and, where deployed, their outputs enter the committed closure; the irreducible residue is cross-hardware bit-portability, handled by record/replay."* Kind stays ABSOLUTE for the "output = f(closure)" core.

**P1-4. AX-10 — NOT-A-CEILING: finite-class mutation kill is exceeded by proof-carrying exclusion of infinite defect classes; Rice misused.**

Quoted: *"adversarial adequacy is certified as 100% mutation-kill over a DECLARED, hash-committed, finite class M … No feasible system can certify refutation-completeness over the open class of all possible defects"*; derivation: *"by Rice's theorem, every non-trivial semantic property of the system's own code is undecidable, so no total procedure can decide…"*

Attack: a machine-checked functional-correctness proof refutes the **infinite** class of all semantics-altering mutants relative to the spec — strictly exceeding any finite committed M for the verified perimeter. Rice bars *deciding* arbitrary code; it does not bar *checking a supplied proof* — proof-carrying admission is a total decidable procedure over (code, proof) pairs. The frontier confirms the mechanism class exists (Lean-verified disproofs, kernel-checked counterexamples). The closing "nothing feasible today exceeds it" is also resource phrasing inside an ABSOLUTE label. Correction: ceiling = *"proof-carrying exclusion of entire defect classes wherever a machine-checked correctness proof covers the seat, plus 100% committed-finite-class mutation kill for the unverified remainder, plus typed-UNKNOWN escalation outside both"* — mutation kill is the floor mechanism for the unproven region, not the global maximum.

**P1-5. AX-20 — same defect as AX-10: "executable measures only" is exceeded by refinement proofs; deciding conflated with proof-checking.**

Quoted: *"Monotonicity over UNMEASURED capabilities is unattainable by any feasible system; the ceiling is exactly non-regression on executable, committed measures"*; derivation: *"a decidable admission gate therefore exists only for capabilities reduced to executable measures (tests, gate counts, proof obligations discharged)."*

Attack: a machine-checked refinement/invariant-preservation proof for step Sₙ→Sₙ₊₁ certifies non-regression over an **infinite behavior class** not reducible to executable measures; the admission gate stays decidable because the candidate arrives with the proof. The corpus's own frontier marks this regime (*"installs self-modifications only after proof-carrying certification of invariants"* — the Gödel-machine line; 'Closing the Loop' verified reward). Correction: *"…non-regression on executable committed measures PLUS machine-checked preservation proofs over spec'd behavior classes, the union monotone"*; the impossibility clause narrows to "capabilities neither measured nor covered by a preservation proof." Kind: keep ABSOLUTE only with this widened formula.

**P1-6. AX-04 — KIND-INFLATED / incomplete enumeration: "only two mechanisms" omits attested-channel capture; corroboration strength is a declared resource.**

Quoted (derivation): *"The only two mechanisms that add origin assurance are (a) verifying a signature … and (b) independent corroboration."*

Attack: a third mechanism is feasible today: **attested-channel capture** — TLS-oracle protocols (DECO/TLSNotary-class) or a TEE-attested fetcher bind the received bytes to the publisher's TLS certificate identity at capture time, transporting origin assurance through the publisher's channel key where the *content* is unsigned — strictly stronger than a bare k-of-1 witness digest and independent of both (a) and (b). Additionally, (b)'s assurance is a monotone function of witness count/independence — a declared deployment resource (which B0 declares NIL) — so the corroboration limb has no absolute maximum, only a resource-parameterized one. Correction: add mechanism (c) to the triple; restate the corroboration bound as parameterized by the declared witness set; either relabel RESOURCE_BOUNDED or scope the ABSOLUTE label to the negative half ("no local computation creates authenticity after the fact"), which is the only genuinely model-independent part.

**P1-7. AX-11 vs AX-15 — KIND-INCONSISTENT: identical storage physics, one labelled ABSOLUTE, the other RESOURCE_BOUNDED.**

Quoted: AX-11 (`ABSOLUTE`): *"durability exactly at the platform's fsync barrier"*, *"for all effects on media the system controls."* AX-15 (`RESOURCE_BOUNDED`): *"On the declared operating model (single node, POSIX filesystem, commodity SSD with honored write barriers)…"*

Attack: AX-11(i) is scoped to the same declared platform (fsync semantics, POSIX rename atomicity) that makes AX-15 RESOURCE_BOUNDED; persistent-memory atomic units, battery-backed write caches, and replication all move the atomicity/durability point, exactly as AX-15 concedes ("nothing short of replication — out of the declared model — exceeds it"). One label is wrong. Correction: relabel AX-11 RESOURCE_BOUNDED, or carve clause (ii) (two-generals / atomic-commitment impossibility — genuinely model-independent) as the ABSOLUTE part and clause (i) as RESOURCE_BOUNDED.

**P1-8. AX-21 — declared-resource premise falsified by the corpus's own frontier.**

Quoted: *"given the declared toolchain: SBCL/Common Lisp … no verified Lisp compiler exists."*

Attack: the verified-stacks frontier in this same corpus records *"Brack (verified Scheme compiler via CakeML, CPP 2026)"* and CakeML itself (bootstrapped, offline-obtainable). "No verified Common Lisp compiler" survives, but a strictly smaller trust residue is reachable **today** by writing the trusted kernel in a verified-compiled substrate (CakeML/Brack target) rather than SBCL — the SBCL constraint is a scope choice, not a limit. RESOURCE_BOUNDED is the right kind, but under the supreme law (rule 3: if less than the maximum is delivered by scope, the superior must be declared) the ceiling text must state: *"a strictly superior transport exists — retargeting the trusted kernel to a verified-compilation substrate (CakeML/Brack) removes the diverse-double-execution mitigation from the compiler link — and is excluded only by the declared SBCL commitment."*

## P2 — overshoot in scoping/phrasing

**P2-1. AX-05.** Quoted: *"verification that the cited authority semantically supports the citing proposition — both are excluded, and any claim to deliver them without human adjudication is structurally dishonest under H1."* Overshoots its own ladder (R3: *"pinpoint text … machine-verified to be … the recorded basis of the proposition"*). For propositions that are **formal objects** of the system, support-verification reduces to derivation provenance: the conclusion's certified derivation uses rule R, whose admission record binds R to pinpoint span S of authority A — decidable, zero-LLM. Correction: scope the impossibility to *NL-proposition-vs-NL-authority* support; formal-proposition support is inside the ceiling, attained by AX-01×AX-04 composition.

**P2-2. AX-02.** Quoted: *"H5+H6 force the plan to exist as a committed finite artifact BEFORE execution (deterministic replay requires a frozen plan…)."* H5 requires the plan be a **pure function of frozen inputs**, not pre-committed: a deterministic planner over a committed finite domain yields replayable synthesized plans. Consequently the ceiling's five-item decidable defect class is undersized: VAL-class precondition/effect validation and sound/complete plan **synthesis with existence certificates** on committed finite domains (the corpus's own formalize-then-solve threat: perfect accuracy at ~10^165 states) is a strictly larger feasible decidable class. Correction: replace "requires a frozen plan" with "requires the plan be a deterministic derivation from frozen inputs," and extend the decidable class to declared-finite-domain STRIPS validation/synthesis, keeping MODEL_RELATIVE.

**P2-3. AX-17.** Quoted: *"a wall-clock claim that is at most 'certified cost × attested machine rate r', where r is … irreducibly attestation-trusted — no system can make wall-clock seconds themselves offline-verifiable with zero producer trust."* Interval **bracketing** by independent time authorities — Roughtime k-of-n (malfeasance-provable), eIDAS qualified timestamps, timestamped witness cosignatures (all in this corpus's frontier) — yields offline-verifiable wall-clock upper bounds on a run against pinned third-party keys: zero *producer* trust, trust relocated to an external quorum. Correction: "*producer*-attestation is not the maximum; the ceiling for wall-clock is k-of-n external-time-authority bracketing, with the residue being trust in that quorum."

**P2-4. AX-19.** Quoted: *"the only feasible route is making it structural — … a local invariant of one seat — the strongest form available without whole-program formal verification of the runtime."* The parenthetical concedes the exceeding route exists (whole-program verification of the mediator perimeter — the corpus's own AX-21 program); Rice bars deciding *arbitrary* code, not verifying constrained code. The ceiling presents the local-invariant form as the maximum simpliciter while its own derivation admits it is resource-bounded on that leg. Correction: state completeness-of-mediation as attainable at two rungs — structural (local invariant, available now) and verified-perimeter (strictly stronger, gated on AX-21 resources) — and note the relativity in the statement, not only in a subordinate clause.

**P2-5. AX-18 (minor, beyond P0-2).** Quoted: *"n·c_commit = the irreducibly SERIAL cost of appending n accountable transitions."* Batched Merkle appends amortize the serial floor to ⌈n/b⌉·c_batch with per-item cost one hash; "irreducibly serial per transition" overstates. Correction: floor is per-*batch* serialization with O(1) amortized hashing per item, which the attainment_mechanism already implicitly uses.

## Summary

No ceiling survives untouched in kind AND statement AND derivation. The systemic defects are three: (1) **H8-circularity** — three ceilings (AX-06/13/18) prove the axiom rather than a limit, and use a false "trusted setup" premise to exclude transparent succinct proofs; (2) **resource-conditioned caps under ABSOLUTE labels** — the witness-quorum family (AX-04/07/12, AX-08(b)) and "feasible today" phrasing (AX-10/13) violate the corpus's own AX-21 labelling standard; (3) **impossibility-by-misapplied-theorem** — Rice/FLP used to cap what proof-*checking* (AX-10, AX-20, AX-19) and partially-synchronous consensus (AX-03) demonstrably deliver. The single most consequential finding is P0-3: unknown_rule(4) as frozen contradicts the closure's own DIM-12 stability family and blocks tournament verdicts that certified invariance would lawfully decide.
