## 2. B0 STRENGTHS ALREADY AT / NEAR SUPREME — the load-bearing foundation E_star keeps

These are the axes where B0's strongest evidence is `PROOF_CARRYING_OR_VERIFIED` or structurally-enforced `SINGLE_WRITER`, and where the mechanism eliminates an error *class* rather than guarding it:

- **AX-06 Provenance / integrity (PROOF_CARRYING).** RFC-6962 Merkle single seat with domain separation + unbalanced split (CVE-2012-2459-safe), inclusion+consistency proofs, and **recompute-and-compare lineage** (`authority-evidence-replay.lisp` trusts no declared root). This is the single most supreme-grade capability.
- **AX-13 Deterministic-artifact integrity / reproducibility (PROOF_CARRYING).** Independent re-verification spine (kernel diversity), L6 verifier staged *inside* the release identity, and a fail-closed gate-plenary manifest whose `:completed t` is positive proof — a crash yields no manifest, never a false green.
- **AX-09 Honest ignorance (PROOF_CARRYING/SINGLE_WRITER).** Fail-hard NFC seat, honest-ceiling judges, and a proof-manifest/census machinery (`verify-proof-manifest.py`, `run-proofs.sh`) that **cannot be gamed into "proved"** — honesty is a first-class, un-droppable artifact (LEVEL7 matrix, `[ANAKLISI ISCHYEI]` retractions).
- **AX-11 Single-writer truth (SINGLE_WRITER, structural).** One authorized RDF writer (`emit-graph`), one public generation seat, and **OS-DAC uid separation** giving the producer a kernel EACCES on the store.
- **AX-12 Accountable transition / constitutional closure (SINGLE_WRITER, structural).** The constitutional barrier is a CLOS `:around` method — **no command can reach its primary method without passing the constitution**; capability closure structurally refuses non-`:trusted` capabilities.
- **AX-19 Introspection (SINGLE_WRITER/PROOF).** Registry-verified provenance traces bound to contract/proof, and MOP visible-thought as a construction invariant ("cannot think invisibly").
- **AX-10 Adversarial self-falsification (SINGLE_WRITER).** Mutation-kill suites that re-introduce every prior finding and kill it; counterfactual ablation; the un-false-greenable honesty gate — a direct realization of the CLAUDE.md internal-adversary law.
- **AX-08 Temporal / anti-rollback (PROOF-leaning).** RFC-6962 consistency proofs recomputed before trust; genTime-floor anti-rollback; bitemporal replayable receipts.
- **No-LLM-in-the-trusted-path (cross-cutting, HONORED EVERYWHERE).** All 8 areas confirm the trusted path is LLM-free; the only LLM (advisor) is admitted at cognition stage-1 decomposition and verified out before trust — a load-bearing invariant E_star must preserve.

## 3. B0 GAPS — axes that are PARTIAL / ABSENT / heuristic and must be lifted to the ceiling

- **AX-15 Durability / crash-consistency — the worst gap.** The core legal-artifact writers (`emit-graph` `:supersede` with no fsync/atomic-rename; `deploy.lisp`) have **no crash consistency**; a power loss mid-write leaves a truncated canonical `.ttl`. The real crash-safe store is honestly **absent-by-design** (Perennial/Coq blocked). Durable guarantees exist only for governance ledgers, not the artifact path.
- **AX-21 Formal verification / property transport — the central proof-debt.** authority-v2 is **0/17 PROVED and 0/13 Level-7**; the kernel K, verified CDDL parser, verified checker, crash-safe store, and 5-link refinement chain are all blocked (F*/EverParse/Coq/Perennial/CompCert absent, network 403). Real proof today is confined to narrow coverage gates (FSM audit, architecture-gate).
- **AX-05 Citation validation — heuristic in loop.** Extraction is regex/search with a ≤120 article cap and PageRank salience; cross-law citations unresolved; two dead/quarantined stubs (`citation-strategy.lisp` constant 1.0, `ai-citation-strategy.lisp`). The authority *chain* is strong; the citation *resolution* is not proof-carrying.
- **AX-01 Legal-reasoning correctness — bounded/heuristic.** The deontic classifier is a linguistic heuristic (~81.5–91.5%); the jurisprudence judge measures co-citation frequency, not correctness; `meta-ontology.lisp` explicitly declares this axis out of scope by design.
- **AX-22 Generality across legal orders — Greece-coupled.** Domain validation hardcodes 120 articles, FEK issues, `/exp/ell`, el/en, and a revision calendar. The cross-legal-order design (`LAWMAX-UNIVERSAL-SOURCE-CONTRACT.md`) is an **unapproved, frozen proposal** with no executable enforcement.
- **AX-04 Origin ingestion — un-attested.** `government-source.lisp` fetches over the network with heuristic HTML/PDF/CP1253 parsing and no signed link from the publisher's bytes; the otherwise-supreme authority chain bottoms out on trust in fetched bytes.
- **AX-10 residual.** Omega's `use-default*` restarts inject empty/synthesized content and continue (silent degradation), and the constitutional gate **fails open** on a predicate error — both contradict the zero-error law and must be closed.
- **AX-03 residual.** The engine `parallel-executor.lisp` swallows per-stage errors and marks stages executed regardless — no transaction/rollback, nondeterministic intra-tier order (also an AX-14 replay break).
