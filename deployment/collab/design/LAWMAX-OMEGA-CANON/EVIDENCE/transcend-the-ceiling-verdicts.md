## Synthesis (bottom line, verbatim substance)

- **No sixth pillar survived.** Every attempt to name an orthogonal trust primitive
  collapsed into a recomposition of Pillars 1–5; the recurring failure signature — each
  idea reused P4 as subroutine, P2 as predicates, P3 as checker, left P1 untouched — is
  the decisive tell of an upgrade, not a new axis. The five-pillar frame is closed under
  these attacks.
- **Four attacks exposed real under-specifications**, each strictly superior on its axis
  and worse on none: Preclusion Control, Interpretive Completeness, Realized-Outcome Loop
  (conditional on the anti-gaming guard), Outcome Sovereignty. All four are now NATIVE
  clauses of the pillar definitions in `02-ARCHITECTURE.md` (not addenda).
- **Honestly rejected:** blocking cross-foundational unanimity (defends against a risk the
  finitary legal computations never incur; kills liveness; enlarges the TCB) — retained
  only as a non-blocking flag for the highest-stakes tier and a preference for a
  foundationally distinct reproduction kernel; the antifragile ratchet (architecturally
  reducible; its headline rides on content volume, which is out of scope) — retained only
  as actor-keyed views + the mandatory prior-perturbation invariance test.
- **The hardest frontier:** the construal generator's declared coverage boundary
  (interpretive completeness) — the space "every construal a court could accept" is not
  decidable (Axiom Υ), so completeness is necessarily coverage-relative, and the honesty
  of the whole weapon reduces to making that boundary structurally un-fakeable and
  empirically tightened over time.
