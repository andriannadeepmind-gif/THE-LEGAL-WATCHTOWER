# 03 — ORGANS (the virtual firm as faculties; multi-model; all untrusted)

Principle: the "employees" of the virtual firm are **capability organs**, not agents with
authority. Many organs; ONE gate (K); ONE world (L2); ONE proof per answer. Elastic scale:
a huge matter opens 5 or 500 instances of an organ — no coordination rot, because organs
do not exchange memos; they all write proposals against the same verified case-world and
K adjudicates. No trusted specialist exists: the "accountant's" number is recomputed; the
"clerk's" deadline is derived from procedural law-as-code.

## Multi-model mandate (specification, not option)

Every generative organ (O2, O3, O5–O9) is a socket bay: N frontier models run the same
brief in parallel, propose competing outputs, and K/the certificate layer adjudicates.
Model identity never appears in the trusted path or its artifacts. Adding a new model =
registering a socket; removing one = closing it; zero redesign either way. This clause is
what turns every future model release, from any vendor, into an automatic upgrade of the
firm.

## The absorption workflow (one-round law, operationalized)

1. Opponent filing arrives → O3 ingests (provenance-sealed).
2. O9 decomposes it into moves/construals; anything novel is flagged.
3. P4 re-runs exhaustion over the enlarged space; P2 coverage stamp updates.
4. Counter is certified, O12 renders it, and the novelty + its counter enter P5 forever.

## Human-in-the-loop points (exhaustive list — nowhere else)

- Critical fact confirmation (O3): the lawyer confirms what a key exhibit shows.
- Open-texture judgment adoption (O2): the lawyer adopts/edits a defeasible position.
- Strategy election (O11 output): the lawyer chooses among certified dominant lines.
- Filing authorization: nothing is filed without the lawyer's sign-off.
Everything else is machine-verified; nothing else is machine-trusted.
