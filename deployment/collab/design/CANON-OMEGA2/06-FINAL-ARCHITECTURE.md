## 3. THE SPLIT-VERIFIER FAMILY (replacing the god-kernel — `autopsy.md` Claim 9 / P-D)

The monolithic `K` is rejected: `autopsy.md` enumerates ≥15 heterogeneous responsibilities spanning six
distinct trust foundations bundled under one name; `reject-A` #1 shows the calculus it embeds is an
unvalidated construal. **Replacement:** a family of small, independently-minimal, independently-proven
verifiers behind **one typed admission bus and one write seat**, so "one seat per concept" is honored
*per concept*, not "one seat for all concepts." A bug in the Horty checker cannot fail-open the deadline
decider. Each verifier and gateway is given the mandatory **5-tuple: seat / interface / invariant /
failure mode / verification method.**

### 3.1 K-adm — structural admission decider (the genuinely tiny kernel)
- **Seat.** `authority-v2/kernel/admission-model.sexp` (the existing v2 admission kernel; the *only*
  member that must reach CakeML/Lean-class minimality).
- **Interface.** `admit(proposal) → COMMIT(record) | REJECT(reason) | UNKNOWN`. Total, decidable,
  deterministic, fail-closed. It dispatches to the specialized checkers below and admits only if the
  required checker(s) return PASS *and* a premise-trust manifest (§5.1) is attached.
- **Invariant (I-ADM).** No path admits a record without (a) the matching checker PASS, (b) a complete
  premise-trust manifest, (c) a coverage/type stamp (K-typ). Nothing "trusted-but-unjustified" or
  "complete-but-unstamped" is representable in the output type. `[DESIGN-ENTAILED; targeted THEOREM]`
- **Failure mode.** Checker error/timeout/ambiguity ⇒ REJECT (fail-closed). Kernel compromise ⇒ full
  bypass (it is in the TCB of every guarantee) → attested boot, signed policy, N-version cross-check.
- **Verification.** Bypass fuzz (zero orphan commits without a PASS+manifest in the ledger); determinism
  replay (bit-identical decision from `(proposal, policy-version, clock)`); N-version diff of the decider.

### 3.2 K-src — recompute-from-source authority verifier
- **Seat.** `source/write-authority.lisp:emit-graph` guarded by `source/validation-authority.lisp`
  (canonical-TTL validate *before* emit) + `source/merkle-authority.lisp`.
- **Interface.** `verify_authority(candidate) → AuthorityRef@interval | REJECT`. Recomputes the
  provenance/ELI key + temporal validity from an authenticated official source; a poisoned document can
  at worst become a *rejected proposal* (`design-A` §9).
- **Invariant (I-SRC).** Every admitted `AuthorityRef` resolves to a byte-identical versioned source
  with a valid in-force interval. `[DESIGN-ENTAILED]`
- **Failure mode.** Amendment/repeal-chain edge case or forged gazette that validates → poisoned/stale
  ref enters trusted (this is A-W3, the largest honest risk to "small TCB").
- **Verification.** Ingest red-team with forged/amended/repealed sources and edge-case amendment chains;
  any that become a trusted ref falsifies I-SRC (`design-A` §16-W3).

### 3.3 K-prf — proof/certificate checker(s), one per proof system
- **Seat.** `source/proof-carrying.lisp` + `source/authority-proof-bundle.lisp`; **one checker per proof
  system** (Lean/LRAT/Horty), each independently reproducible — *not* one blob.
- **Interface.** `check(certificate, proof_system) → VALID | INVALID`. A conclusion is trusted only over
  the *formalizable fragment* and only with a checked certificate; the solver is removed from the TCB
  (`formal-boundaries` §1c: LRAT checked by `cake_lpr`). SMT steps are A2 only where a certificate is
  produced and checked; elsewhere the solver is quarantined to R3 (`formal-boundaries` §6.3 BLOCKING).
- **Invariant (I-PRF).** Trust bottoms out at a small, independently cross-checked verification core —
  **not** "at mathematics" (`autopsy` Claim 11 correction). Every proof ships **with** its
  formalization-fidelity artifact (§5.2); the proof is worthless without it.
- **Failure mode.** *(This is where `reject-A` #1 lives.)* A certificate is VALID over a **calculus that
  mis-models Greek legal defeat** (e.g. conforming-interpretation-as-support where CJEU *contra legem*
  requires disapplication). The proof checks; the conclusion is legally wrong; no UNKNOWN fires because
  the mis-encoding *is* the failure to recognize the choice.
- **Verification.** N-version the checker AND N-version the **calculus itself** (independent
  implementations of the defeat semantics; diff on a benchmark of decided cases — `design-A` §16-W8);
  the calculus is a first-class formalization-fidelity artifact (§5.2), subjected to the dual-formalize
  + back-translate + human-ratify apparatus A applied only to leaves. **Residual (BLOCKING-adjacent,
  disclosed):** benchmark validation of the calculus is empirical spot-checking over an open
  fact-pattern space — it *narrows*, never *closes*, `reject-A` #1. Stated, not claimed away (§9, R-1).

### 3.4 K-typ — coverage-stamp & four-valued type enforcer
- **Seat.** New R1 seat on the admission bus (co-located with `deployment/verify/gate-registry.sexp`).
- **Interface.** `stamp(conclusion) → {PROVED|REFUTED|UNKNOWN|STABLE} × coverage-stamp × ⟦A|F|Ev|Scope⟧`.
  Refuses unstamped-complete; enforces the `formal-boundaries` composition rule **A-level never upgrades
  F-level** (a proved deadline arithmetic over an unattested legal mapping is `⟦A4|F1|…⟧`, never
  "verified deadline").
- **Invariant (I-TYP).** No conclusion crosses to R0/output without a four-valued tag, a C-relative
  coverage stamp, and a four-field assurance stamp whose overall strength = the *weakest* field.
  `[DESIGN-ENTAILED]`
- **Failure mode.** Mode-laundering — an open-texture judgment presented as a computed fact (the most
  dangerous legal-AI failure, `design-C` §8). Detected probabilistically by mode classification, which
  is itself fallible (C-item 10).
- **Verification.** Seed open-texture questions phrased to look computational; measure mis-tag rate that
  survives review (`design-C` weakness 10); assert every emitted claim carries a resolvable evidence
  pointer.

### 3.5 K-write — single-writer + witness-quorum commit seat
- **Seat.** `source/journal.lisp` (append-only, SHA-256 hash-chained) + `source/self-history.lisp`.
- **Interface.** `commit(record, quorum_cosign) → appended | REJECT`. The *only* physical writer to the
  authoritative log; witness-quorum cosigns.
- **Invariant (I-WRITE).** Append-only, tamper-evident, externally anchored (multi-TSA); an altered past
  entry breaks all subsequent roots and the external anchor (`security-privilege` I-AUD-1). `[THEOREM for
  the chain; the external anchor reduces trust to "not all TSAs collude," EMPIRICAL, disclosed]`
- **Failure mode.** Insider coalition spanning the full separation-of-duties boundary + suborning a
  majority of external anchors (`security-privilege` R4/R5 residual).
- **Verification.** Tamper test (mutate a past entry ⇒ chain+anchor verification fails+alerts);
  orphan-effect test (zero privileged effects without a ledger entry).

### 3.6 K-precl — preclusion / safety-game certificate checker
- **Seat.** New R1 seat adjacent to `systems/orchestrator-cli/*-gate.lisp`; consumes drafting-time
  safety-game fixpoints.
- **Interface.** `check_preclusion(certificate) → PASS | REJECT`, PASS only with (i) named frozen
  rule-set version + world assumptions, (ii) an **abuse-of-right / unfair-terms self-attack** (does the
  foreclosure itself create a GCC 281 voidability handle?), (iii) a **future-law sensitivity** flag for
  mandatory-law domains, and (iv) the **client-non-self-binding proof** (`autopsy` Claim 8).
- **Invariant (I-PRECL).** No preclusion certificate is admitted as "born won"; it is admitted only as
  **"maximally pre-precluded, C-relative, world-W₀, subject to defeasibility and future-law risk"**
  (`autopsy` Claim 8 §6 — the demolition of "born won").
- **Failure mode.** Frozen-world safety game reopened by supervening acts / future mandatory-law
  amendment; over-aggressive clause struck as abusive under GCC 281 (`autopsy` Claim 8 §2).
- **Verification.** Adversarial re-opening tests (inject tolling/waiver/amendment events); assert every
  certificate carries the three flags + the non-self-binding proof.

### 3.7 THE THREE SEPARATE GATEWAYS (must NOT live in any kernel)

`autopsy.md` P-D / BLOCK-2: publication review is **non-decidable and human-in-the-loop by mandate** and
must be a *separate* fail-closed pipeline, never folded into a "total, decidable, deterministic" kernel.

#### G-pub — Publication Gateway (the only private→public egress; fail-closed)
- **Seat.** Separate R1 pipeline over write-once staging (Z4), distinct code paths from internal drafting

- **Interface.** `may_publish(artifact) = scope_bound ∧ privilege_cleared ∧ dlp_clean ∧

  Ordered stages; ABSTAIN ≡ FAIL. Stage 3 includes **explicit steganographic-exfiltration heuristics**

- **Invariant (I-PUB).** Publication occurs **iff** the monotone conjunction holds; the publisher's only

#### G-inf — inference-boundary / egress gateway (per-data-class posture)

## 4. THE EPISTEMIC LAYER — labelled multi-world authority store

Rejecting single-world "the only truth" (`autopsy` Claim 10 / P-E: a `norm → single meaning at t` store
cannot hold equal-rank authority splits, plural construals, alternative fact-worlds, or contested
procedure). The store is a **Legal Position** as a structure, not an answer (`design-A` §1.2):

```
Position(Q) = ⟨ AF, W, A, Λ ⟩
  AF : argumentation framework (ASPIC+-style: strict + defeasible rules, preference ordering,
       attack relation from rebut/undercut/undermine)
  W  : set of admissible factual world-theories (client-theory, opponent-theory, court-found),
       each with an evidentiary support record under burden/standard — NOT a scalar probability
  A  : authority-lattice slice (partial order + defeasible priority meta-norms lex superior/
       specialis/posterior), CONFLICTS FIRST-CLASS ("split: N means X per forum f₁ vs ¬X per f₂")
  Λ  : labeling (Arg × Semantics × Stance × World × Posture) → {IN, OUT, UNDECIDED}
```

- **Conflicting authorities** are *edges, not deletions*; the family emits the *set* of resolutions each
  meta-norm ordering yields, and UNDECIDED + the competing orderings where the meta-norms themselves
  conflict. EU primacy and conforming-interpretation are cross-order attack/support edges.
- **Competing construals** of one norm coexist with authority weights that are *arguments, not scalar
  confidence*. **No stance is privileged by default.**
- **Alternative factual worlds** are named and reasoned over *in parallel*, never merged into "the
  truth"; contested procedural states are typed **DISPUTED**, not derived.
- **Discretion is irreducible and surfaced:** any conclusion whose derivation passes an unresolved
  `DiscretionNode` is emitted `CHOICE-DEPENDENT`, enumerating options + reasons, requiring a recorded R0
  decision before it is settled *for that matter*. The verifier family **refuses to collapse it**
  (`design-A` §7.6). *(This is why A beats C on the discretion axis — `reject-C` consequence (a): A's
  refuse-to-label is structurally stronger than C's tag-and-hope.)*

### 4.1 The four reasoning modes — tagged, never laundered
Each conclusion records the mode that produced it; **no conclusion may launder its mode** (`design-C`
§8 cross-mode rule):
1. **Computational law** (deadlines, thresholds, arithmetic): strict rules, decidable, K-prf-checked,
   `⟦A3/A4 | F≤F3 | … ⟧` — the proved *arithmetic* never launders the *legal characterization* (which
   period applies is F≤F3, `formal-boundaries` §4.1).
2. **Open texture** (good faith, proportionality, καλή πίστη, GCC 281 abuse): defeasible rules + penumbra
   markers; the family maps the argument space and *refuses to compute* the answer; reserved to R0.
3. **Precedent** (analogical, defeasible): HYPO/CATO-style factor/dimension analogy with distinguishers
   surfaced; retrieval *proposes* cases, the analogy is checked for form — status ≤ HYPOTHESIS until R0
   adopts. Greek lower courts are **not bound by precedent** (`beat-the-canon` LA-2) — precedent is
   persuasive material, never a trusted "in-force" fact.
4. **Evidence** (world-construction under burdens/standards): πιθανολόγηση / πλήρης απόδειξη are
   qualitative thresholds, **not** calibrated probabilities; scalar probability is refused on the trusted
   path (`design-A` §7.4). *(The optional calibration spine, §7, adds a separately-verified probabilistic
   channel — HYPOTHESIS status — that never enters the deductive labeling.)*

**5-tuple for the epistemic store:**
- **Seat.** `source/version-graph.lisp` (bitemporal, 114-defun) + `authority-v2/` store + a new
  `multi-world` typing over norms/facts/procedure.
- **Interface.** `position(Q, O, t, s, w, p) → ⟨AF,W,A,Λ⟩ + defeaters-that-would-flip`.
- **Invariant.** Collapsing to a single world/stance is an *unrecorded decision* the type system forbids
  (`design-A` Corollary §1.2); conflicts and DISPUTED procedure are first-class; bitemporal (valid-time
  + record-time) so "law as of the act" and "law now" are both queryable.
- **Failure mode.** Disagreement-graph inconsistency — an incoherent conflict-graph yields incoherent
  cross-world reasoning (`design-C` weakness 4).
- **Verification.** Consistency audits over the authority/interpretation graph; injected contradictory
  authorities must be *surfaced*, not silently absorbed; formalize a battery of doctrine-heavy decided
  cases and measure representational failures (`design-A` §16-W10, the deepest falsifier).

### 5.1 Premise-trust ledger (`autopsy` Claim 1)
- **Seat.** New R2 seat, hash-chained into `source/journal.lisp`; attached to every trusted emission.
- **Interface.** `manifest(emission) → {fact-confirmations by whom; formalization artifacts + validation
  status; coverage stamp; unchecked trust dependencies enumerated}`.
- **Invariant.** No trusted emission without a checkable derivation **and** a complete typed manifest of
  what the derivation is *trusting* — INV restated honestly: it guarantees "checkable derivation + full
  manifest of trusted premises," never "cannot emit a wrong trusted claim" (`autopsy` Claim 1 §6).
- **Failure mode.** A mis-formalized statute produces a machine-checked proof of a legally false
  proposition; the manifest makes the trust *visible* but does not make the premise *correct*.
- **Verification.** Assert every trusted emission carries a resolvable manifest; seed known-wrong
  formalizations and measure ratifier catch-rate (`design-A` §16-W2).

### 5.2 Formalization-fidelity as a first-class, human-attested, versioned artifact
- **Seat.** New R2 artifact shipping **with every proof** (and — the `reject-A` #1 repair — with the

- **Interface.** `fidelity(φ) → ⟦F-level | isomorphism trace | back-translation diff | named ratifier |
  scope + temporal validity + re-review trigger⟧`, F0→F3, F3 the ceiling (`formal-boundaries` §5).
- **Invariant.** A-level never upgrades F-level; legal-conclusion correctness is **never** stamped
  THEOREM; the artifact expires on statutory amendment / new CJEU/ECHR/Άρειος Πάγος ruling in scope.
- **Failure mode.** Ratifier rubber-stamps, or back-translation is misleading, or an omission (a whole
  exception never encoded) survives because there is nothing to back-translate (`formal-boundaries` §3.2).
- **Verification.** Dual independent back-translation reconciled; N-version formalization cross-diff
  (agreement is weak evidence; disagreement provably reveals a gap); contrastive near-boundary test pairs.

### 5.3 Procedural-state machine + deadline kernel + independent liveness watchtower
- **Seat.** `firm-operations` §2 deterministic engine + `design-B`/`design-C` fail-**loud** watchtower,

- **Interface.** `deadline(procedure_family, trigger_event, trigger_date, residence, service_mode,

- **Invariant.** Deadlines fail **loud, not silent** (availability is a correctness requirement); a

  models August suspension (ΚΠολΔ 147§7), holiday rollover (144§2), residence-dependent terms, retroactive
  rule versions.
- **Failure mode.** **Formalization/characterization gap** — right arithmetic on the *wrong* procedure
  family / trigger (`firm-operations` F12); dual-engine disagreement (BLOCKING incident, earliest date
  operative pending resolution). And the fail-closed-vs-deadline collision (`security-privilege` W6 /
  `design-C` kill-shot 2).
- **Verification.** Held-out decided procedural questions with known answers; `AT-DL-*` acceptance tests;
  fault-injection: kill the authority-retrieval path and assert the deadline alarm still fires from the
  independent calendar (`design-B` W6).
- **The deadline-vs-fail-closed resolution (adopted, NOT C's break-glass μπάλωμα):** on a fail-closed
  block near a deadline, the DENY is fast + loud, escalates to a named human on a bounded timer, and the

  is **never** auto-downgraded (`security-privilege` §9.4; `firm-operations` §9.4). `reject-C` kill-shot 2

### 5.5 BLOCK-1 resolution — cross-matter consistency via a minimal-disclosure compliance seat

- **Interface.** `consistency_check(new_assertion) → {consistent | contradiction | needs-human}` computed

- **Invariant.** The guard never discloses walled content: it operates over commitment *identifiers +

- **Failure mode.** The de-identified commitment representation leaks a walled position via
