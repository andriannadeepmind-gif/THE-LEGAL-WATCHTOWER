# RESOLUTION R-2 — The honesty tax (advocacy-register vs INV)

**Team:** RESOLUTION TEAM R-2
**Target:** CANON-OMEGA2-ARCHITECTURE.md §9 R-2 (+ §7 calibration spine); tournament.md B-2 / D3;
design-A-epistemic.md (INV, DiscretionNode, §7.6); beat-the-canon.md Exploit 4 + Part 4 (CA-3);
formal-boundaries.md (typed-emission lattice A0–A4 / F0–F3).
**Hypothesis under test:** R-2 is a **CONFLATION** dissolvable by a **two-register architecture** —
(i) an internal-belief channel (honest, INV-governed) vs (ii) an advocacy-artifact channel (the filed
brief, a permitted zealous presentation, NOT a truth emission).
**Binding forbidden move:** "solved because we redefined the problem." A resolution that only RELABELS
or MOVES the contradiction is not a resolution. This document hunts that failure in its own proposal.

---

## 0. The contradiction, stated exactly (no softening)

The corpus states R-2 as an *un-absorbable, empirical* BLOCKING contradiction (tournament B-2; canon §9):

> To beat honest hedging on the discretion axis you must assert arguable positions at full lawful
> confidence (non-fabricating, no false authority, but *not volunteering* your own uncertainty). That
> requires a two-register split (internal-belief honest under INV **+** advocacy-output optimizing
> realized forum payoff) whose advocacy layer, by construction, **emits beyond what it can justify —
> the exact class INV makes unrepresentable.** You cannot hold both single-gate honesty and calibrated
> aggression; adopting the latter makes the system "a different animal."

INV (design-A; beat-the-canon Exploit 4): the system is *architecturally incapable of emitting an
unjustified trusted claim*; §4's four-valued discipline makes *confident guessing unrepresentable*;
P3 bars any softening of open-texture and any LLM between certification and emission. Because every
firm output is routed through the single certificate gate K, there is **no primitive for calibrated
aggression** — the advocate's staple of asserting an arguable position at full confidence and making
the opponent spend to rebut it. `[the contradiction is DESIGN-ENTAILED against INV as stated]`

The precise conflict is **not** mission-vs-INV. It is: *one emission gate is asked to enforce the
truth-conditions of two different speech acts at once.*

---

## 1. The diagnosis — a genuine TYPE conflation, named in the corpus itself

beat-the-canon Exploit 4 already isolates the conflation verbatim: the incumbent **"conflates two
distinct things: *trusted internal belief* (which SHOULD be justified) and *advocacy assertion* (which
lawfully may exceed what one can prove, so long as it is arguable and non-fabricated)."** The report
treats this as an *un-closable* fault only because the incumbent's *single gate* makes the conflation
**structural** ("single-gate honesty ... is its whole identity", Part 4). That is the load-bearing
observation: **R-2 is not a contradiction inside the mission; it is a contradiction manufactured by
routing two emission TYPES through one gate.** The honesty tax is the *shadow of a missing type*.

The dissolution claim therefore has real teeth (it is not a wording trick) because the two speech acts
have **independently-recognised, genuinely different consumers, truth-conditions, and failure classes**:

| axis | Type-T trusted emission | Type-V advocacy artifact (the filed brief / oral submission) |
|---|---|---|
| illocutionary force | assertion-of-verified-truth | a **motion** — "here is my client's best lawful case; I invite the tribunal to adopt it" |
| consumer | a party who **relies on it as true** (the lawyer's own reasoning, client advice-as-law, a fact stated as established) | a tribunal **institutionally constituted NOT to take it as verified truth** — it decides; the opponent contests |
| truth-condition | *justified* (checkable derivation + complete premise-trust manifest) | *non-frivolous ∧ reasonably arguable ∧ non-fabricated* |
| verification | the certificate gate K (internal) | **the adversarial process itself** (opponent + judge) |
| failure class | relying party harmed by unjustified reliance | **misleading the court** (a candor / professional-conduct breach) — *a different failure class* |

Law (the adversarial system presupposes the brief-as-motion), linguistics (illocutionary force), and
ethics (Κώδικας Δικηγόρων: the duty is *zealous representation within the bounds of law*, **not**
candid disclosure of one's private credence) each certify that these are two types, not one relabelled.
Applying INV — a discipline whose whole point is "no wrong claim reaches a consumer who relies on it as
verified truth" — to a Type-V artifact that **has no such consumer** is a **category error**. `[DESIGN-
ENTAILED: the two types have distinct, independently-attested felicity conditions]`

## 2. The typed separation (the resolution architecture)

**Two disjoint emission types, two disjoint gates. INV governs T only.**

- **Type T — Trusted Emission.** Anything asserted *as true / verified* to a relying consumer: an
  internal legal conclusion the lawyer will rely on, advice stated to the client as *the law*, a fact
  stated as *established*. **Gate:** the INV certificate gate K — *no Type-T emission without a
  checkable derivation AND a complete manifest of its unchecked trust dependencies* (autopsy Claim 1
  honest form). Uncertainty is surfaced as UNKNOWN/CHOICE-DEPENDENT (design-A §7.6). Unchanged.
- **Type V — Advocacy Artifact.** The filed brief (προτάσεις), motion, oral submission. **Gate:** the
  *advocacy discipline* of §3 — **NOT** INV. It may advance arguable-but-unproven positions a Type-T
  emission never could, precisely because it is typed as a motion, not a truth.

**Why INV governs T and not V — the principled reason (not a convenience).** INV exists to make ONE
error class structurally impossible: *a wrong claim reaching a consumer who relies on it as verified
truth.* A Type-V artifact structurally cannot instantiate that class, because its consumer (the
tribunal) is constituted to not rely on it as verified truth and the opponent is charged with
contesting it. Enforcing INV over V would be disciplining a speech act by the truth-conditions of a
*different* speech act — the exact category error whose *structural* form is the honesty tax. Removing
the honesty tax = **deleting the category error**, i.e. giving V its own gate. `[DESIGN-ENTAILED]`

**Structural type discipline (the anti-laundering rails, both directions):**
- **V ↛ T.** The internal trusted store **never ingests the firm's own filed positions as trusted
  facts.** This is exactly the *client-non-self-binding / K-preclusion* guard already in the canon
  (autopsy Claim 8). A confident advocacy surface cannot be laundered back into internal belief.
- **T → V permitted, one-directionally.** A Type-V artifact MAY rest on Type-T verified facts and
  resolved authorities; it may ALSO add arguable positions T could not carry. The internal T-channel
  retains the full UNKNOWN/weakness topology *for the lawyer*; what V does is **not publish it**
  (lawful non-volunteering, beat-the-canon l.69). CA-6 concealment is concealment of the *advocacy
  surface only* — it never corrupts internal belief. So the "internal honesty launders into a
  confident public surface" sub-charge fails: honesty of T is preserved; V simply is not a
  publication of T's confidence topology, and the tribunal is not a relying consumer of that topology
  (surfacing the weakness is the *opponent's* burden).

## 3. The advocacy channel's OWN discipline (so Type-V is governed, not lawless)

This is the section that decides whether the split is a real dissolution or a **relabel-and-escape**.
If V could emit *anything* confident and ungoverned, we would merely have renamed "confident guessing"
as "advocacy" — the forbidden circular move. Three disciplines prevent that; the first ELIMINATES a
sub-class, the second CONTAINS, the third routes the irreducible remainder to a human.

1. **Non-fabrication floor — ELIMINATED (structural, A2/fail-closed).**
   - *Every citation* in a Type-V artifact must resolve to a **byte-identical versioned authority**
     (design-A T1(ii): "every `AuthorityRef@interval` resolves to a byte-identical versioned source").
     No non-existent / mis-cited authority can appear. Fail-closed.
   - *Every fact asserted as established* must trace to a Type-T verified fact or an admitted record.
     Facts lacking that trace may appear **only** in explicitly hypothetical / *arguendo* framing,
     never as asserted established fact — a typed, checkable distinction.
   - **Consequence:** fabrication, false authority, and "asserting-an-untraceable-fact-as-established"
     — a whole sub-class the corpus lumped into R-2 — are made **structurally impossible**, not merely
     forbidden. `[ELIMINATED — error class structurally unrepresentable]`

2. **Arguability gate — CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED (checkable at the extremes, human in the penumbra).**
   A Type-V position must be *non-frivolous / reasonably arguable*. The argument-space engine (A's AF +
   B's L5 discretion map) can **check structural non-frivolousness**: the position corresponds to at
   least one *admissible* argument in the framework — an instance of an admitted inference rule over
   *resolved* authorities, even if defeasible. That check is decidable and measurable (argument-space
   membership). It **cannot** decide the open-textured middle ("is this arguable *extension* good-faith,
   or misleading by selective framing?") — that is a discretion node (§4). *Honest inheritance:* the
   non-frivolousness *check* inherits R-5's F≤F3 ceiling — a mis-encoded defeat-semantics could mislabel
   a frivolous position as structurally arguable; contained by N-version calculus diffing, not closed.
   `[CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED; membership-checkable, F≤F3 on fidelity]`

3. **Human signature — the emission act (R-DR-3).** A Type-V artifact is EMITTED only by the partner's
   **cryptographic, non-repudiable sign-off bound to the exact version hash** (firm-operations R-DR-3;
   post-signature edit re-opens the gate, R-DR-2/AT-DR-1). The machine proposes the *survivable-but-
   unproven region* and (where the class is thick, §7) a **calibrated forum-outcome forecast**; the
   **human** exercises the aggression choice and the candor-line judgment and signs. The discretion node
   lands exactly where the canon already puts every discretion node (design-A §7.6 CHOICE-DEPENDENT +
   B2 human authority). `[DESIGN-ENTAILED placement; the human act is the load-bearing gate]`

## 4. HEAD-ON: does the split SMUGGLE the contradiction back in?

**The strongest form of the attack (the task's central demand).** "You say V is not a truth emission,
so INV does not govern it. But the advocacy channel must *select which arguable position to push*, and
'this framing has high P(adopted ∨ unrebutted)' is a **calibrated-confidence** judgment. Either (a) INV
governs that selection and you are back inside the honesty tax, or (b) INV does not govern it and you
have built an **ungoverned channel that emits unjustified confident claims** — the exact failure class
INV existed to prevent, now merely renamed 'advocacy.' Heads I win, tails you lose."

**Answer — the dichotomy is false because it collapses THREE objects that must stay typed apart:**

- **A calibrated forum forecast** ("under this bench, on this balancing test, P(adopted) ≥ 0.7 with
  guaranteed ≥90% coverage") is **not** a trusted-truth claim about *the law*. It is a claim about a
  *forum outcome*, and it is **honest precisely because it is calibrated and carries its coverage** —
  this *is* the §7 conformal spine, which the canon itself says is "a quantified honest answer with a
  machine-checkable coverage THEOREM" that "threads the honest-refusal-vs-dishonest-confidence binary
  the corpus assumed was un-threadable." **INV never forbade calibrated forecasts.** INV forbade
  *uncalibrated confident guessing wearing a trusted-truth stamp.* A calibrated forecast is the honest
  **opposite** of the forbidden object. It also already sits, permitted, in the untrusted proposer zone
  (design-A l.191: a probabilistic model "is a proposer heuristic ... never enters the trusted
  labeling"; A0/A1 in the formal-boundaries lattice).
- **The advocacy assertion in the brief** ("the contract is void for X") is neither a trusted-truth
  claim nor a forecast; it is a **motion**, whose felicity condition is *non-frivolous ∧ arguable ∧
  non-fabricated*, not *true* and not *calibrated*.
- **The link between them is a human-signed discretion act** (R-DR-3), not a machine emission.

So the actual pipeline is:
`[calibrated forum forecast: honest, untrusted, §7-verified] → [human reads forecast + mapped argument
space] → [human exercises aggression + candor judgment, SIGNS] → [advocacy assertion emitted as a
MOTION under the signature]`.
At **no** step is an *uncalibrated confident claim emitted with a trusted-truth stamp*. The forbidden
object — INV's target — **appears nowhere in the pipeline.** What appears is (i) an honest calibrated
forecast (permitted, indeed the honest ideal) and (ii) a human discretion node (permitted, the canon's
standard device). The contradiction is therefore **not smuggled back** into R-2's original form: horn
(a) fails (the selection terminates in a human-signed decision, never a Type-T emission); horn (b) fails
(V is *not* ungoverned — §3's floor + gate + signature govern it, and the confident-guessing sub-class
is ELIMINATED, not renamed).

**Where the attack is nonetheless RIGHT — the genuine, smaller residue it correctly locates.** The
calibrated forecast is only as honest as its calibration holds, and calibration rests on
**exchangeability** of the legal reference class — which §7 itself flags as EMPIRICAL and possibly
false (law is non-stationary). On a **novel** point — the very *survivable-but-unproven* region CA-3
most wants to mine — the reference class is thin, so §7's anti-gerrymandering guard forces the forecast
to **UNKNOWN**. There the human gets *no calibrated arming* and must make the aggression call on pure
professional judgment. So the smuggling analysis does **not** resurrect R-2, but it correctly pins a
residue at the **intersection of R-2's aggression node and R-1's novel-at-speed node**: a novel
aggressive framing at a one-shot node, decided by the human, with no calibrated support and no next
round. That residue is real and un-eliminable — and it is *human*, not machine (§5, Residue-3).

**Why this is a DISSOLUTION and not a MOVE.** A pure relabel would (i) leave the fabrication sub-class
intact under a new name, (ii) add no new honest capability, and (iii) hide the residual judgment where
no one signs for it. The split does the opposite on all three: (i) fabrication/false-authority is
**structurally ELIMINATED** (strictly smaller scope than R-2 originally lumped together); (ii) the
calibrated-forecast arming is a **new honest capability** that did not exist; (iii) the residual
judgment is routed to a **named, signed human discretion node** — REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT is a *defined
honest terminus*, not a hiding place. The one thing the split does **not** buy — and the document
**refuses to claim** — is that the retyped system *wins*. It buys *lawful, honest capability to
advocate*; whether that beats a bare elite human is §5 Residue-2.

## 5. The residue, stated honestly (NOT claimed away)

- **Residue-1 — the candor line. `REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT`.** The boundary between zealous advocacy and
  *misleading the court* (candor to the tribunal; ΚΔ / Κώδικας Δεοντολογίας) is **open-textured** and is
  a **professional-conduct discretion node**. The machine checks the non-fabrication floor (ELIMINATED)
  and structural non-frivolousness (CONTAINED); the *good-faith-arguable-vs-misleading* penumbra —
  including selective framing / non-volunteering shading into misleading-by-omission — is irreducibly a
  human judgment, surfaced and routed to the signature. A top human advocate faces **exactly** this
  line; the system does not out-resolve it — it makes it explicit and signable. *Not eliminated.*
- **Residue-2 — the out-win empirical question. `STILL-EMPIRICAL / UNKNOWN` (the surviving core of R-2).**
  Whether the two-register, machine-armed human **actually out-wins** a bare elite human on the
  discretion axis is EMPIRICAL and currently UNKNOWN. It turns on Axiom Φ's fit to the real forum (how
  imperfect real challenge is). The split makes the system *lawfully capable* of full-confidence
  advocacy — removing the incumbent's actual defect (radical honesty *forbidding* advocacy, the
  self-handicap of tournament D3 / beat-the-canon l.69) — but it **does not prove** the armed human
  beats the bare human. The §7 spine improves the arming where the class is thick; it cannot prove
  superiority (autopsy Claim 12: backtesting can never by itself reach "empirically dominant"). *Neither
  side may claim it proven.*
- **Residue-3 — selection at a thin/novel class. `REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT`, intersecting R-1.** Where the
  forum-outcome class is thin/novel (CA-3's target region), §7 forces the forecast to UNKNOWN; the human
  makes the aggression call unarmed, at a possibly one-shot node with no next round (couples to R-1
  novel-at-speed). *Not closed.*
- **Inherited: Residue on the arguability check. `CONTAINED (F≤F3)`.** The non-frivolousness check is
  only as faithful as the AF calculus (R-5); a mis-encoded defeat-semantics could green-light a frivolous
  position. Narrowed by N-version calculus diffing + benchmarking; never THEOREM.

## 6. The mandatory 5-tuple (component: the two-register advocacy channel)

- **Seat.** A new **advocacy-artifact typing** over emissions: the existing K certificate gate becomes
  the **Type-T** gate (unchanged, INV-governed); a new **Type-V advocacy gate** = `non-fabrication
  floor` (byte-identical `AuthorityRef` resolution, reusing design-A T1(ii) + `authority-v2/`) ∘
  `structural-arguability check` (AF/L5 argument-space membership) ∘ `R-DR-3 partner signature bound to
  version hash`. The forum-forecast arming is the §7 conformal spine (separate R1/R2 seat, output never
  enters Λ). V↛T rail = the client-non-self-binding / K-preclusion guard (existing).
- **Interface.** `advocate(position, matter) →` (a) *admit* iff every citation resolves byte-identical
  ∧ every asserted-established fact is Type-T-traceable ∧ position ∈ admissible-argument-space, then
  attach `forum_forecast(position) → prediction-set|UNKNOWN` (§7), then **require partner signature**;
  (b) else *block* with the specific missing predicate. The signed bytes are the only thing filed
  (firm-ops R-FIL-2 pre-flight). Internal belief remains queryable as Type-T with full UNKNOWN topology.
- **Invariant.** *No Type-V artifact is filed without (i) zero unresolved citations, (ii) zero
  untraceable facts asserted as established, and (iii) a partner signature on the exact bytes.* **INV
  (no unjustified Type-T emission) is untouched.** V never becomes a Type-T input (V↛T rail). A
  calibrated forecast is never emitted as a Type-T truth-claim and a Type-T conclusion is never softened
  into V's confidence-concealed surface without preserving the internal UNKNOWN record.
- **Failure mode.** The candor penumbra (Residue-1): a position that passes the non-fabrication floor
  and structural-arguability check may still *mislead by selective framing* — an open-textured breach
  the machine cannot adjudicate; escapes only through a human who signs a misleading brief (insider /
  rubber-stamp risk, tournament D10; reduced, not eliminated). Secondary: a thin-class UNKNOWN forecast
  leaves the aggression call unarmed (Residue-3, couples R-1). Tertiary: AF-calculus infidelity
  green-lights a frivolous position (R-5 inheritance).
- **Verification.** (a) *Floor:* injected non-existent citations and untraceable "established facts"
  must be blocked, 100%, fail-closed (adversarial suite). (b) *Arguability:* argument-space membership
  audited against a labelled bench of frivolous vs arguable positions; false-admits measured. (c)
  *Forecast:* reliability diagrams / Brier / log-score on held-out, matter-disjoint realized outcomes;
  thin classes must yield UNKNOWN not a gerrymandered set (§7). (d) *Signature:* version-hash binding +
  post-signature re-open (AT-DR-1). (e) *The out-win claim (Residue-2):* only pre-registered, live,
  adversarial trials of armed-human vs bare-human may speak — and none has run; until then UNKNOWN.

## 7. The 5-disposition tuple + honest status label

Decomposing R-2 across the binding taxonomy (each sub-claim → exactly one disposition):

**⟨ DISSOLVED · ELIMINATED · CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED · REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT · STILL-EMPIRICAL ⟩**

1. **DISSOLVED** — the core contradiction "INV forbids the advocacy assertion." It was a *conflation of
   two emission types* (T vs V) forced together by the incumbent's single gate. Separated cleanly: INV
   is a discipline on Type-T (trusted-truth to a relying consumer); the brief is Type-V (a motion, no
   such consumer). INV never *should* have governed V. The two types have independently-attested
   distinct consumers, truth-conditions, and failure classes — so this is a real separation, not a
   relabel.
2. **ELIMINATED** — fabrication / false authority / asserting-untraceable-fact-as-established: made
   structurally impossible by the non-fabrication floor (byte-identical authority resolution +
   fact-traceability, fail-closed). A whole sub-class R-2 had lumped in is gone.
3. **CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED** — structural non-frivolousness (argument-space membership) + calibrated
   forum forecast where the class is thick (§7) + version-hash-bound signature audit. Measurable;
   fidelity capped at F≤F3 (R-5 inheritance).
4. **REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT** — the candor-line penumbra and the aggression choice at thin/novel classes.
   Surfaced and routed to a signed human discretion node; *identical* to what a top human advocate faces.
   Not machine-resolved; couples to R-1 at the novel one-shot node.
5. **STILL-EMPIRICAL / UNKNOWN** — whether the two-register armed-human **out-wins** the bare elite
   human on the discretion axis. Un-provable by design; turns on Axiom Φ's fit to the real forum.

**Honest overall status label:**
> **R-2 as a CONTRADICTION is DISSOLVED** (it was a type conflation; the honesty tax was the shadow of a
> missing advocacy-emission type). **R-2 as an EMPIRICAL BET is NOT PAID** (STILL-EMPIRICAL). The split
> is therefore **de-BLOCKING but not victory-proving**: it removes the *logical* contradiction between
> the mission and INV and eliminates the fabrication sub-class, while its irreducible remainder is a
> *human* professional-conduct discretion node (REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT) plus the un-provable out-win
> question (EMPIRICAL). The forbidden circular move — "solved because we redefined the problem" — is
> explicitly *declined*: the retyping is legitimate (two independently-recognised speech acts) **and it
> does not claim the system wins** — it claims only that the system may now advocate *lawfully and
> honestly*, which is a capability the incumbent structurally lacked, not a proof of superiority.

**One-line for §9:** R-2 downgrades from **BLOCKING (un-absorbable contradiction)** to **DISSOLVED
contradiction with a REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT candor residue and a STILL-EMPIRICAL out-win residue** —
the honesty tax is no longer a *paradox*, only an *unproven bet* and an *irreducibly human line*.
