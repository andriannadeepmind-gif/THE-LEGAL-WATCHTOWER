## R-1 · Novel dispositive move at a zero-recess one-shot node

**(1) Claimed resolution (one sentence).** Separate the impossible event P₁ (the *system* machine-certifies
a novel move at speech latency — left exactly as impossible) from P₂ (the *client* takes the best lawful
action), add omitted branch (c) — an untrusted `UNCERTIFIED-SUPPORT` artifact that never crosses gate K and
is handed to the human advocate who bears the call — and shrink the residue with exhaustive pre-enumeration
of `Q×I×M` and lawful Greek time-manufacture devices (σημείωμα-προθεσμία, αναβολή, 691Α opposition).

**(2) Smuggle-hunt.**
- *Circular / redefinition?* No. P₁ is held **exactly as impossible** as the canon proved; nothing is
  weakened. What is split is the canon's welding of "system emits UNKNOWN" to "client concedes" — an actual
  conflation, because the actor at the node was always the human advocate (HA-1), never a system emission.
  Separating a genuine conflation is dissolution, not redefinition. The resolution's own verification (§6.iv)
  *mandates the residue be reported non-zero* — a built-in anti-circularity tripwire. Passes.
- *Does branch (c) smuggle back the forbidden branch (a)?* Branch (a) was a *weaker sub-certified emission*.
  Branch (c) adds **no gate** — it routes speed-sensitive work *outside* K and stamps every artifact
  UNCERTIFIED with an unchecked-dependency manifest. The machine's analysis does reach the forum through the
  advocate's mouth, but nothing is stamped *verified*; this is the same structure as an associate handing a
  partner a memo. So the forbidden class ("system emits an unjustified *trusted* claim") is made unreachable,
  not merely forbidden. **The one genuine leak it introduces is automation bias (D10)** — a flustered human
  treating UNCERTIFIED as certified — which the resolution honestly labels CONTAINED, not eliminated. No hidden
  smuggle beyond that admitted one.
- *Over-claim to watch:* "exhaustive enumeration of `Q×I×M`" leans on the reachable product being finite/
  enumerable; its open-texture face (defeat-semantics variants, characterization) is exactly R-3/R-5
  territory and is NOT enumerable. The resolution concedes this — rows 2–3 (concealed fact, genuinely-novel
  idea) survive — so the over-claim is disclosed, not smuggled.

**(3) Honest final status: REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT** (with a DISSOLVED framing layer, CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED
tail). The *system-side* invariant contradiction is genuinely gone: K never has to certify novelty at speed
and uncertified support is not a trusted emission. The "UNKNOWN=concede" and "zero-recess" premises are
dissolved conflations (much of "one-shot" is under-prepared procedure a σημείωμα window converts). But the
**operative** question the founder cares about — does the client *win* a genuinely-novel dispositive move
when no recess is obtainable — is not solved; it is lowered to exactly what an elite unaided advocate faces.
Claiming more would be the circular sin; the resolution correctly refuses it.

**(4) Exact irreducible residue + comparison.** Residue = nodes where the move is genuinely novel (outside
enumerated `Q×I×M`, not produced by any available generator = the **R-3 population floor**) **or** turns on a
fact concealed until the instant (**LA-5**), AND the court refuses every lawful recess device (κατεπείγον /
immediate 691Α / hard on-the-spot preclusion), so the human decides on uncertified support alone — plus the
contained automation-bias sub-residual. Formally `(R-3 ∪ LA-5) ∩ (court-refused true-zero-recess)`.
Comparison: **BETTER-THAN** the top human team on enumeration/retrieval/one-shot-detection/speed (real
DESIGN-ENTAILED gains); **EQUAL-TO** on the deepest axis (pure-novel legal judgment, concealed-fact ambush,
and certified correctness at true zero recess — *no certificate exists for any party there*). Net on the
irreducible core: **EQUAL**. Not smaller, not larger — the definition of the human limit.

## R-2 · The honesty tax — advocacy-register vs INV

**(1) Claimed resolution (one sentence).** R-2 is a **type conflation**: route two independently-recognized
speech acts through two disjoint gates — Type-T (asserted-as-verified-truth to a relying consumer, INV-
governed, unchanged) and Type-V (the filed brief = a *motion*, not a truth, governed by a non-fabrication
floor + structural-arguability check + a partner's version-bound signature) — so INV never governs the
advocacy artifact and the "honesty tax" is the shadow of a missing emission type.

**(2) Smuggle-hunt.** This is the resolution most exposed to "solved by redefining the problem," so attack it
hardest.
- *Is the T/V split a verbal relabel?* No — the distinction is legally, linguistically, and ethically real
  and pre-existing: a partner filing προτάσεις is not swearing an affidavit; illocutionary force, consumer,
  truth-condition, and failure class genuinely differ. A brief IS a motion-to-adopt, not an assertion-of-
  truth. A relabel would leave the fabrication sub-class intact and add no capability; this split
  **structurally eliminates** fabrication/false-authority/untraceable-fact-as-established (byte-identical
  authority resolution + fact-traceability, fail-closed) and adds a real new honest capability (calibrated
  forum forecast). That is dissolution, not wordplay.
- *The heads-I-win horn (the central demand):* selecting which arguable position to push uses a
  confidence-like judgment; either INV governs it (back in the tax) or it is an ungoverned confident channel.
  The resolution's answer holds: a **calibrated** forum forecast *with coverage* is the honest *opposite* of
  INV's target (uncalibrated confidence wearing a truth-stamp), and the selection terminates in a **human-
  signed** discretion act, not a machine emission — so the forbidden object appears at no step. Accepted.
- *Where it DOES smuggle, uncharitably:* the claim that the tribunal "structurally cannot instantiate the
  reliance-harm class because it is constituted not to rely" is **too strong**. Tribunals can be misled;
  adversarial rebuttal is imperfect — that imperfection is *literally* Axiom Φ. The split offloads the
  correctness guarantee onto the adversarial process whose reliability is the unproven empirical unknown. So
  the *logical* contradiction dissolves, but the *safety* of dissolving it rests on an unpaid bet. The
  resolution flags this as Residue-2 (out-win STILL-EMPIRICAL) — it does not hide it — so it is honest, but
  the founder must not hear "dissolved" as "safe/winning."

**(3) Honest final status: DISSOLVED (as a contradiction) — the genuine dissolution of the three.** The
logical impossibility ("single gate cannot hold both honesty and calibrated aggression") was manufactured
entirely by routing two types through one gate; give V its own gate and it vanishes, with independent
grounding, not a trick. What is explicitly **not** dissolved: (i) the candor-line penumbra (zealous vs
misleading-the-court) → REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT; (ii) whether the armed human actually *out-wins* the bare
elite human → STILL-EMPIRICAL/UNKNOWN (Axiom Φ; un-provable by backtest). So: **R-2 the CONTRADICTION is
dissolved; R-2 the BET is unpaid.**

**(4) Exact irreducible residue + comparison.** Residue = the human's candor-line judgment and the aggression
call at a **thin/novel** reference class (where §7 forces the forecast to UNKNOWN and the human decides
unarmed — coupling to R-1), plus the un-provable out-win question. Comparison: **BETTER-THAN** the human team
on non-fabrication (structural elimination a human can only try to observe); **EQUAL-TO** the human on the
candor line and the unarmed thin-class aggression call (a top advocate faces exactly this — the machine makes
it explicit and signable, does not out-resolve it); out-win = **UNKNOWN** (may not be claimed better). Net on
the discretion core: **EQUAL**.

## R-3 · Correlated-model false-consensus on a population-shared blind spot

**(1) Claimed resolution (one sentence).** Refusing elimination outright, contain in three: (1) a **non-model
deterministic recompute** strips the fact/citation/in-force/quotation/deadline/consistency face from
admissibility **even at N = the whole population** (agreement is never an admission predicate); (2) route the
open-texture *applicability/judgment* face to the **one oracle outside the model population** — a Greek-law
expert judging **blind to the model consensus**, cross-school where stakes warrant; (3) an **effective-
independent-members** metric makes the blind spot *visible and confidence-capped* on ground-truthable probes.

**(2) Smuggle-hunt.**
- *Circular / relabel?* No, and it says so first: it refuses ELIMINATION and refuses DISSOLUTION (R-3 is a
  real ceiling, not a conflation). §1 does *actual* non-model work; §2 hands the unverifiable core to a
  defender genuinely outside the pretraining distribution; §3 declares exactly where the metric goes blind
  (no-ground-truth open-texture probes). Nothing is moved and called solved.
- *The de-gating move — legitimate?* "The containment does not depend on independent model foundations
  existing, because its catchers are a deterministic recompute and a human, independent by construction."
  This is legitimate and correctly neutralizes the canon's UNKNOWN — but note it only *relocates* the whole
  weight of the R-3 core onto **the human oracle**, since the recompute is blind on the open-texture face by
  the formalization-gap THEOREM. So R-3's real bite reduces entirely to "a human, possibly cross-school,
  judging blind." That IS the human limit — no smuggle, but also no more than the human.
- *The back-door smuggle it catches on itself:* if the model consensus leaks to the human, the oracle
  **anchors** and correlates *into* the population (I-ORACLE → rubber stamp) — R-3 re-enters through its own
  containment. Mitigation = mandatory blinding; blinding effectiveness is EMPIRICAL (§5b), not proven. Named,
  not hidden.
- *The seam where it becomes WORSE than a human (the sharpest finding):* the reduction to the human limit
  holds **only if every open-texture item is actually routed to the oracle.** The tertiary failure — K-typ
  **mode-mislabels** an open-texture judgment as computational/A-axis — skips the oracle and **launders a
  whole-population wrong idea into a trusted PROVED stamp** a human team would never have applied. That is
  strictly WORSE than a human team, and it rides on **R-5** (verifier/mode-tag fidelity F≤F3, "the single
  most dangerous residual because it is quiet"), which is itself still BLOCKING. The resolution acknowledges
  the R-5 inheritance but cannot close it.

**(3) Honest final status: CONTAINED-AND-MEASURED + REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT** (the resolution's own label,
and it is honest) — **conditional on R-5.** A sub-class (fact/citation/arithmetic/consistency, incl. the
resolvable face of the canonical GreekBarBench article-ID failure) is ELIMINATED-as-*admission*. The
open-texture judgment core is lowered to the shared-doctrine blind spot every human team carries and made
visible where ground truth exists. But "reduced to the human limit" is true *only where the mode-tag is
correct*; at the R-5 mislabel seam the system is WORSE than a human team.

**(4) Exact irreducible residue + comparison.** Residue = a proposition on which (i) the whole model
population is wrong the same way, (ii) the error is open-texture *application* yielding a real/in-force/
byte-identical authority — so no recompute, proof-check, arithmetic, dominance test, or metric fires — and
(iii) the firm's own expert(s), judging blind, hold the same prevailing-doctrine reading; magnitude UNKNOWN,
estimable only by a second independent-school panel, never closable. Comparison: **BETTER-THAN** the human
team on the formalizable face (stripped even at N=all); **EQUAL-TO** on the prevailing-doctrine open-texture
core (the human is the final judge in both); **WORSE-THAN** at the R-5 mode-mislabel edge (laundered PROVED
stamp). Net on the addressed judgment axis: **EQUAL**, with a WORSE tail contingent on the unclosed R-5.

## CLOSING VERDICT

**Which is which, of the three:**
- **Genuinely DISSOLVED — R-2.** The honesty tax was a real type conflation (assertion-of-truth vs motion-to-
  adopt); giving the brief its own gate removes the *logical* contradiction with independent legal grounding,
  and structurally eliminates the fabrication sub-class. It is the only one of the three whose central
  contradiction actually stops existing. Caveat: dissolving the paradox is not winning the forum — the
  out-win stays EMPIRICAL and the candor line stays human.
- **REDUCED-TO-HUMAN-LIMIT — R-1 and R-3.** Neither is dissolved; both are honestly lowered to exactly the
  ceiling a top human team faces (R-1: pure-novel judgment at true zero-recess; R-3: the prevailing-doctrine
  open-texture blind spot). Both do strictly BETTER than the human on the formalizable/retrieval axes and
  EQUAL on the irreducible core. Neither claims to make the client *win* the residue — the correct, non-
  circular refusal.
- **Which stays STILL-BLOCKING?** None of R-1/R-2/R-3 *as its own contradiction* remains blocking under the
  resolutions' DESIGN-ENTAILED rails. But the smuggle-hunt shows all three reductions are **conditional and
  not yet DEMONSTRATED**: R-1 and R-3 both inherit **R-5** (mode-tag / verifier fidelity — still BLOCKING,
  and the seam where R-3 turns WORSE-than-human), R-1 assumes the **R-4** fail-closed gate that canon §9 says
  is *not yet discharged* (currently fail-OPEN), and R-2 rests on the unpaid **Axiom-Φ** bet. So the honest
  three-way answer: R-2 dissolved, R-1 and R-3 reduced-to-human-limit — and the residual blocking has
  *migrated* from these three headline contradictions down into R-4 (fail-closed gate) and R-5 (calculus/
  mode fidelity), which now carry the load.

**The single most important honest sentence for the founder:** *The three deciding contradictions are no
longer paradoxes — R-2 genuinely dissolves and R-1 and R-3 are lowered to exactly the ceiling a top human
legal team also faces (better than that team everywhere except the irreducible novel-judgment / shared-
doctrine core, where it is equal) — but every one of those reductions is DESIGN-ENTAILED, not yet
DEMONSTRATED, and it holds only if three still-open seats close: R-4 (the admission gate is actually fail-
closed, not the inherited fail-OPEN), R-5 (the mode-tag never mislabels an open-texture judgment as proved —
the one seam where the system can be worse than a human), and the honest acceptance that beating the forum
(Axiom Φ) is an unproven bet, not a delivered result.*
