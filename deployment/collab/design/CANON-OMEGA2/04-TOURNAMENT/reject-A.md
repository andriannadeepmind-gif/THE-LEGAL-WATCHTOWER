# REJECT-A — Skeptical Rejection Team report on Design A (Epistemic First-Principles)

**Target:** `design-A-epistemic.md`
**Mandate:** find the single fatal, unrecoverable flaw. Do not design, do not improve, do not credit. Only attack.
**Method:** hold the design to (a) its own stated axioms (§0, N1–N5, Corollary §1.2, §1.3), and (b) the binding conditions — especially "Deadlines/latency/availability/reliability ARE correctness requirements", "Goal: maximum superiority over any AI or human legal team", "No fabrication", and the rescue clause "cost/time/compute/staffing are NOT constraints".

Design A is unusually honest: §15–16 pre-concede the formalization gap, completeness undecidability, replay weakening, insider residue, and latency. A pre-conceded flaw is still a flaw; an *honestly declared* unrecoverable limitation is still unrecoverable. The kill below is therefore built from the design's own theorems, not from anything it failed to mention. The murder weapon is Corollary §1.2 and the §0 method note, turned against the one layer the design exempts from its own honesty apparatus: the Kernel's calculus.

---

## 1. THE THREE STRONGEST KILL-SHOTS

### KILL-SHOT #1 (FATAL) — The Kernel's calculus is a silently-privileged construal of contested meta-law, sitting inside the TCB with zero trust control, and this violates the design's own founding theorem.

**The design's own load-bearing commitments, verbatim in spirit:**
- §0: "If the representation is honest… the architecture is largely forced by it; if the representation is dishonest (a single truth-world, facts asserted rather than proven, **one interpretation privileged silently**), **no amount of engineering recovers correctness**."
- Corollary §1.2: "collapsing to a single world/stance is **not a simplification but an unrecorded decision** — it hides N3/N4/N5." (THEOREM.)
- N4: there is **no total order on authorities**; `lex superior/specialis/posterior` are themselves defeasible meta-norms that conflict.
- N5: discretion/open-texture are **irreducible choice points**; any system that resolves them silently is **fabricating**.
- §2/§4 flagship claim: the Kernel "does not need to *know law*; it checks **form**" — this is the *entire* justification for "small TCB" and for "errors structurally impossible rather than forbidden" (§14).

**The contradiction.** The Kernel cannot compute a single labeling without a *fixed* encoding of exactly the things N4/N5 declare contested and irreducible:
- the **attack relation** — whether, e.g., conforming interpretation (σύμφωνη ερμηνεία) is a *support* edge or whether EU primacy forces *disapplication* (a different edge) (the design flags this exact choice as W1);
- the **strict-vs-defeasible compilation** — which norms compile to strict rules vs. defeasible-with-penumbra (§7.1);
- the **discretion-node marking** — *which* nodes are irreducible CHOICE points that the Kernel refuses to collapse (§7.6);
- the **meta-norm priority encoding** — how `superior/specialis/posterior` conflicts are enumerated (§7.2).

Each of these is not "form." Each is a **contested construal of Greek/EU meta-law**, chosen once by the builder, **applied silently and uniformly to every matter**. The design surfaces *object-level* interpretive competition (literal vs. teleological; world A vs. world B) — but it **hard-codes the meta-level construal** and never surfaces it as a CHOICE, never ratifies it, never carries a competing calculus, never back-translates it. By the design's *own* Corollary §1.2, hard-coding a contested construal is "not a simplification but an unrecorded decision" that "hides N3/N4/N5" — i.e. it is, **by the design's own definition, dishonest**. And by §0, once the representation is dishonest at a layer, "no amount of engineering recovers correctness."

So the Kernel — the sole component the design labels TRUSTED and the sole component that assigns labels — commits *precisely the sin the design defines itself against*, at the deepest and most load-bearing layer. The honesty apparatus (dual-formalization §7.3, human ratification §7.3, back-translation §7.3, discretion-gate §7.6, N-version audit W8) is lavished on the **leaf** formalizations (per-premise, per-source) and is **entirely absent for the root** formalization (the calculus itself). §13 governs kernel *change* but "boundary invariants" there are the *security* properties (fail-closed, complete mediation) — **not the legal faithfulness of the calculus**. There is no seat in the design that validates that the Kernel's theory of Greek legal defeat is faithful to Greek law. The one formalization whose error is **global, uniform, and invisible** (W8) is the one formalization with **no ratification, no back-translation, no adversarial gauntlet, no CHOICE-surfacing** — the exact opposite of the treatment the design says such an object demands.

**Exact failing scenario (green light on a legally wrong conclusion).**
A matter turns on the interplay of a Greek national provision with an EU directive. The Kernel's encoded calculus models conforming interpretation as a *support* edge (national norm re-read to align with the directive). In *this* matter, CJEU doctrine (the *contra legem* limit on conforming interpretation) means conforming interpretation is **unavailable**, and the correct structure is **disapplication under primacy** — a different edge yielding a different downstream labeling. The Kernel, applying its fixed semantics, labels the conforming-interpretation argument IN, the disapplication argument OUT, and — fatally — **never marks the availability question as a discretion node**, because the mis-encoding *is* the failure to recognize the choice. Now trace the honesty apparatus:
- Premises are correct; sources resolve to byte-identical `AuthorityRef@interval`. ✔ passes §7.3.
- Dual independent formalization of the *premises* agrees (the premises aren't the error). ✔ no diff.
- Back-translation renders the premises faithfully to the ratifier (the premises are faithful). ✔ ratifier signs.
- The discretion-gate does not fire (the calculus didn't classify this as discretion). ✔ no CHOICE emitted.
- The Kernel emits a **"trusted, sound, well-labeled" Position that is legally wrong** — uniformly, invisibly, under a green light.

This is exactly the error class §14 promised was "structurally impossible rather than forbidden." It is neither impossible nor even detected: it is produced *by* the trusted core, *through* the intact honesty apparatus, because the apparatus was never pointed at the layer that failed.

**Why unrecoverable within this design.** The design is caught on two horns, and both are inside its own axioms:
- **Horn A (keep it hard-coded):** then the trusted core silently privileges one construal of every meta-legal relation for every matter — dishonest per Corollary §1.2, and per §0 "no engineering recovers correctness." The flagship "structural error-elimination" claim (§14) is false for the dominant, most damaging error class.
- **Horn B (pluralize the calculus — carry competing attack-relations / defeat-semantics and surface disagreement as the design does for object-level stances):** two failures. (1) The set of reasonable legal construals of the defeat relation is **open-ended and not enumerable**; any *finite* encoded set is *itself* a silent choice of which orderings are admissible — N4 dishonesty reduced in degree, structurally identical in kind. (2) Where the competing calculi are made plural enough to be honest, they **disagree on exactly the hard cases** (that is what makes those cases hard), so the Kernel emits UNDECIDED/CHOICE for essentially every contested matter → the "trusted conclusion" is **empty precisely where value lives**, converting calculus-dishonesty into universal abstention (which then triggers Kill-shots #2 and #3 everywhere).

A proposed repair — "add calculus ratification + adversarial gauntlet + benchmark for the Kernel's legal semantics" — does **not** recover the design either: benchmark-validation of the calculus is *empirical spot-checking over an open space of fact-patterns*, never a proof of faithfulness (it is the same formalization gap re-instantiated at the calculus level, and undecidable/unbounded because the fact-pattern space is open). That is a **guard around a possibly-wrong schema** — the exact move CLAUDE.md ("φρουρός γύρω από λάθος σχήμα < εξάλειψη της κλάσης σφάλματος") and §14 ("structurally impossible rather than forbidden") deride — just relocated up one level to the most load-bearing seat. The design cannot both (i) keep the small, law-ignorant, form-only Kernel that its §2/§4 argument depends on, and (ii) validate the legal calculus the Kernel unavoidably embeds. The two are mutually exclusive. That is the internal contradiction, and it is fatal to the design's distinctive thesis.

### KILL-SHOT #2 (decisive against the mission) — The architecture guarantees SOUNDNESS; superiority is decided by COMPLETENESS; the design's guarantees are orthogonal to its goal, and it can never mark the gap as UNKNOWN.

**The gap.** The Propose/Check split (§2) buys soundness: nothing is labeled IN that shouldn't be, *relative to the arguments handed to the Kernel*. It buys **zero** completeness: no guarantee, no bound, and — critically — **no honest UNKNOWN-flag** that the decisive argument, authority, or defeater was *not among the proposals*. The design concedes this (§1.3(a) recall is aspirational; W4; C2 "completeness undecidable" THEOREM).

In adversarial litigation, superiority over the opponent is decided **at the margin by the one decisive move the other side missed** — the winning defeater, the overlooked authority, the un-pleaded preclusion. A system that is perfectly sound but omits the one winning argument **loses the matter**. The Kernel cannot help: it validates soundness of what it is given, cannot conjure the missing argument, and **cannot even tell you it is missing** — "the set of all admissible arguments" is not enumerable and the Kernel has no oracle for it. So the system's honesty guarantee is only over *what it considered*; the client consumes *what exists*. The design is honest about the former and blind to the latter, and it will emit a clean, well-labeled, green-lit Position **silently missing the winning move**, with no UNKNOWN raised, because it does not know the move exists.

**Exact failing scenario.** Opposing counsel (human or AI) raises a decisive undercutting defeater — say a specific ECHR-conform reading that flips a proportionality node — that none of the proposer swarm's per-stance clusters generated. The Kernel never saw it, so the delivered Position labels the firm's argument IN with full confidence and no completeness caveat. The firm files on that basis and is defeated at hearing. Post-mortem (W4 detection: differential-coverage audit) finds the missing argument — *after* the loss. Detection is not closure.

**Why this bites the mission (condition 6) specifically.** The entire distinctive machinery of Design A — TCB, Kernel, labeling, ratification, the whole epistemic edifice — is aimed at **soundness/safety**. The competitive metric (finding the decisive move) lives **entirely in the untrusted proposer layer**, which is (a) exactly the part that is *not* the design's distinctive contribution (any design can run many LLMs), and (b) the part with **no floor, no guarantee, no honest ignorance flag**. So the flagship architecture is aimed at the wrong target relative to its own goal: it maximizes what it can guarantee (soundness) while the goal (superiority) is won on the dimension it structurally cannot touch (completeness). C2 makes this a THEOREM-level limitation, not an engineering to-do.

**Why unrecoverable within this design.** Completeness of argument search is admitted undecidable (C2, THEOREM). The design therefore cannot provide any architectural guarantee, bound, or even honest UNKNOWN-flag on the completeness axis. Its only offered response (W4) is a *post hoc empirical audit*, which cannot run inside the deadline of a live matter and cannot certify completeness even after the fact. There is no move within the Propose/Check architecture that closes an open, undecidable search — that is what "undecidable" means.

*Adversary's own concession, stated so as not to over-claim:* a human elite team also cannot guarantee completeness, and the adversarial proposer swarm may improve coverage over a single human. So #2 alone does not *collapse* the design — it demonstrates that the design's guarantees do not reach its goal, and that "trusted output" carries a silent, unbounded, unflagged completeness hole. Combined with #1 (the "trusted, sound" output is only sound relative to an *unvalidated* calculus), the two together hollow out the value proposition: the delivered Position is neither guaranteed-complete **nor** guaranteed-legally-sound — only *derivationally* sound under an unaudited theory of Greek law, with a silent omission gap on the axis that decides matters.

### KILL-SHOT #3 (structural ceiling) — Fail-closed honest ignorance vs. externally-imposed deadlines: on deadline-bound novel/discretion questions the machine is architecturally forbidden to out-perform an unaided human, and can leave the client worse-positioned.

**Setup, with the rescue clause neutralized.** Condition 3 ("cost/time/compute/staffing NOT constraints") *defuses* the throughput objection — you may throw unlimited humans and compute at ratification and discretion. I therefore do **not** rely on bottleneck. But condition 4 makes **deadlines a correctness requirement**, and litigation deadlines are **externally imposed** (court filing windows, appeal deadlines, §7.5 preclusion edges) — they are **not purchasable with more staff**, and adding people to a single novel legal judgment does not collapse the deliberation time it genuinely needs (and some hard questions have no determinate answer reachable in the window at all).

**The bind.** Design A routes every discretion-laden node (§7.6: the Kernel *structurally refuses* to collapse discretion and requires a recorded B2 human decision) and every not-yet-ratified formalization (§7.3) through a mandatory human decision point, and emits BLOCKING/UNKNOWN otherwise (§10, fail-closed, "τίμια άγνοια"). For a genuinely novel question arriving near a hard external deadline, the machine's output at the wire is: *"here is the structure; a human must decide the discretion node / ratify the formalization."*

Now compare the competitor the mission names — an elite human team at equal data and equal procedural position. On the same clock, the human team **exercises professional judgment, commits to its best defensible position, and files** — which is **lawful and non-fabricating** (professional judgment on an open question is not fabrication), and it **preserves the client's rights**. Design A's Kernel **structurally forbids the machine from committing** to a discretion resolution (that would be an LLM/agent in the trusted path — barred by §2 and CLAUDE.md "κανένα LLM στο trusted path"). So at the deadline the machine can, at best, hand the *same* discretion back to the *same* humans — who, if they cannot fully deliberate in time, now guess **without** the machine having decided anything on the load-bearing point.

**Exact failing scenario.** Novel proportionality question, 48h to a non-extendable filing deadline, answer necessarily passes an unresolved discretion node. Design A surfaces CHOICE-DEPENDENT with options and reasons — genuinely useful marshalling — but **cannot** deliver a *conclusion*. If the human authority cannot converge in 48h, fail-closed yields "no position," and the firm either files a human guess (Design A's machinery contributed structure, not the decision) or misses the window. Either way, on this class of cases Design A is **at best equal to** the unaided elite human on the decision that mattered, and never superior — the outcome is capped by the human, which is precisely what the mission asked the machine to beat.

**Why unrecoverable within this design.** To be *superior* on discretion, the machine would have to commit to a discretion resolution itself — i.e. put an LLM/heuristic in the trusted path, forbidden by §2, §7.6, N5 ("resolving discretion silently is fabricating"), and CLAUDE.md. The design has *elevated N5 into an architectural refusal*, which is defensible as anti-fabrication but has a hard price: it **caps the machine's ceiling at the human's judgment** on exactly the highest-value, discretion-laden work where the mission demanded dominance. The design cannot lift the cap without doing the forbidden thing. This is a genuine structural ceiling against condition 6 (and a live risk against condition 4 on non-extendable deadlines), and it is inherent, not incidental. It wounds rather than independently collapses — but it removes the mission's payoff on the very cases that justify building the system.

## 2. VERDICT

**FATAL-FLAW.**

The fatal element is **Kill-shot #1**: by the design's own Corollary §1.2 (THEOREM) and §0 method note, silently privileging one construal of a contested legal relation is *dishonest representation*, and "no amount of engineering recovers correctness" once the representation is dishonest. The Kernel — the sole trusted, sole label-assigning component — **necessarily embeds exactly such a silently-privileged construal** (the attack relation, the strict/defeasible split, the discretion-node marking, the meta-norm priority) and gives it **none** of the honesty controls (ratification, back-translation, dual-formalization, CHOICE-surfacing) the design mandates for lesser formalizations. The design thus commits, at its trust root, the precise sin it defines itself against, and its own §0 says this is unrecoverable by engineering. The escape routes both fail on the design's own axioms: hard-code → dishonest per §1.2; pluralize → either still-silent finite choice (N4) or universal abstention that forfeits the mission (condition 6). The flagship claims — "small, law-ignorant, form-only TCB" (§2/§4) and "errors structurally impossible rather than forbidden" (§14) — are **false for the dominant error class** (calculus mis-modeling), which is global, silent, and passes the intact honesty apparatus under a green light.

Kill-shots #2 (soundness-guaranteed, completeness-decides, undecidable per C2) and #3 (fail-closed vs. external deadlines caps the mission ceiling at the human) are not independently collapse-grade, but they compound #1: the "trusted" Position the system emits is, on close reading, *derivationally sound only relative to an unaudited legal calculus (#1), with an unbounded silent omission gap on the axis that decides matters (#2), and structurally incapable of superiority on deadline-bound discretion (#3)*. The value proposition survives none of the three intact.

## 3. IF A MERGE-AUTHORITY OVERRULES THIS VERDICT — the single most dangerous residual and its cost in a real matter

If the design is nonetheless carried forward (e.g., by ruling #1 a "known limitation to be benchmarked, W1/W8"), the **single most dangerous residual is the un-validated Kernel calculus producing a global, invisible, green-lit legal error** — the #1 scenario. Its danger is maximal precisely because it is the *quiet* failure: unlike a proposer omission (local, one matter, sometimes caught by a partner's instinct) or a deadline miss (loud, visible), a mis-encoded defeat semantics or a mis-marked discretion node is **uniform across every matter that touches that doctrine**, carries the system's **highest trust label**, and **suppresses the very UNKNOWN/CHOICE flag** that is the design's entire safety story — because the error is the failure to recognize the choice.

**Cost in a real matter:** a systematically mis-encoded conforming-interpretation / primacy edge (or a mis-classified proportionality node) means every affected Position is confidently, "verifiably" wrong. The firm relies on it, advises the client, files, and — because the label was TRUSTED and no UNKNOWN was raised — **no human is prompted to look**. The loss is (i) the matter itself (adverse judgment, a client's substantive rights, or a time-barred claim on the §7.5 preclusion path), (ii) **every other open matter sharing the doctrine**, silently mis-advised in the same way until an *external* event (a loss at hearing, opposing counsel, a CJEU ruling) exposes it, and (iii) the firm's professional-liability and credibility exposure for having marketed "machine-verified, structurally-sound" conclusions that were sound only relative to an unaudited theory of the law. The design provides no in-band detector for this class; W4/W8 detection is post-hoc, empirical, and cannot run inside the matter. That is the residual, and it is the one most likely to be discovered by an adversary or a court rather than by the system.
