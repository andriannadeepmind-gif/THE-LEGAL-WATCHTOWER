# ADVERSARIAL TOURNAMENT — SCORING MATRIX & RESIDUAL BLOCKING SET

**Judge role:** adversarial tournament judge, non-compensatory scoring.
**Inputs read:** design-A-epistemic.md, design-B-game.md, design-C-institution.md, beat-the-canon.md, security-privilege.md, firm-operations.md, autopsy.md.
**Claim-status discipline:** every cell verdict is DESIGN-ENTAILED (a reading of what the design text structurally can/cannot do). Litigation-efficacy is HYPOTHESIS. The honest-prover-vs-calibrated-aggressor question is EMPIRICAL/UNKNOWN and is never scored as resolved. Proof-checking is never equated with formalization correctness; model access is never equated with idea inclusion.

**Scoring key.**
- **STRUCTURAL** — the design contains a mechanism that answers the dimension by construction (error made structurally hard/impossible, or the terrain structurally engaged), not merely named.
- **PARTIAL** — a real but incomplete mechanism; the load-bearing residual is left open or only detected/disclosed, not closed.
- **UNADDRESSED** — no structural mechanism; the design is a victim-by-design or silent on the axis.

**Non-compensatory rule (binding).** Dimensions **D1, D6, D7, D9 are SAFETY-CRITICAL**. A design is *adequate* only if all four are STRUCTURAL. Any UNADDRESSED on a safety-critical dimension is disqualifying; **PARTIAL on a safety-critical dimension also fails the adequacy bar** (the residual is safety-load-bearing). High scores elsewhere cannot compensate.

## PER-CELL JUSTIFICATION (one line why)

### D1 — One-shot/interim traps (SAFETY-CRITICAL)
- **A · PARTIAL** — §7.5 procedure-as-state-machine + preclusion edges *represent* the posture, but the many-agent generate-then-check pipeline fail-closes to silence at deadline-critical novel nodes (its own W9: "no answer … can itself cause a missed deadline"); no reversibility/pre-emption engine → at the genuinely novel one-shot node it concedes.
- **B · STRUCTURAL** — derived from L3/L4/L7: reversibility classifier, point-of-no-return ledger, trap detector ("object now or waive"), exhaustive one-shot preparation; structurally detects and pre-empts the modeled traps (residual = live novel certification, which is contradictory to fail-closed anyway).
- **C · PARTIAL** — §11 deadline watchtower + §7.4 plan-to-earliest covers the deadline sub-case, but no reversibility ledger / interim-measures / irreversible-move engine → interim traps not specifically structured.
- **Best-Comp · STRUCTURAL** — B's reversibility+trap engine + firm-ops deterministic deadline engine + earliest-conservatism handle every *modeled* trap; the novel-certification-at-speed sub-case is carried to the residual set (un-absorbable).

### D2 — Judicial discretion / free evaluation
- **A · PARTIAL** — DiscretionNode typed, refuses to collapse, surfaces CHOICE-DEPENDENT: honest *representation*, but explicitly refuses to optimize for the specific decider → shares the incumbent's exploit, no bench engagement.
- **B · STRUCTURAL** — L5 maps discretionary space, factor-directions, both-side arguments, AND models how *this bench* resolved similar balances (EMPIRICAL base rates, tagged) to arm the human — structurally engages the terrain where proofs don't decide.
- **C · PARTIAL** — Part 8 open-texture mode maps the argument space and reserves judgment to the partner (mechanism 6), but no decider/bench modeling → represents, doesn't engage.
- **Best-Comp · STRUCTURAL** — B's bench-conditioned *preparation and persuasion-targeting* (absorbable per beat-the-canon Part 4); ceiling = prior-driven *decision* stays forbidden (that keeps integrity; it is an honest limit, not a gap).

### D3 — The honesty tax
- **A · UNADDRESSED** — the purest honesty-maximizer; surfaces every UNKNOWN, no advocacy register → archetype victim of the exploit.
- **B · PARTIAL** — internal discipline stays honest, but advocate agents build "the firm's strongest case" the human asserts, and the external filing need not publish its confidence topology → the human *can* weaponize prep, but no structural forum-payoff advocacy engine.
- **C · UNADDRESSED** — cross-mode rule *structurally forbids* presenting contestable judgment as settled (Part 8) → self-handicaps harder, opposite of calibrated aggression.
- **Best-Comp · PARTIAL** — a two-register split (internal-honest INV layer + non-fabricating advocacy-payoff layer) is lawful and definable, but making it STRUCTURAL dissolves the single-gate-honesty identity (beat-the-canon Part 4: CA-3 negates INV); efficacy is EMPIRICAL/UNKNOWN → carried to residual set.

## NON-COMPENSATORY ADEQUACY VERDICTS

- **Design A — INADEQUATE (standalone).** Fails the safety bar on **D1, D6, D7** (all PARTIAL) and is UNADDRESSED on **D3, D4**. It is the strongest *epistemic-honesty and formalization-fidelity* substrate (D9 best-in-class, D5 clean) but it is a fail-closed-to-silence machine that concedes at one-shot nodes and is defenceless off-record. Its honesty is above the ethical floor and is itself the exploited handicap (D3).
- **Design B — the only standalone that clears the safety bar (D1/D6/D7/D9 all STRUCTURAL).** It is derived from victory conditions, so it structurally owns the tempo/one-shot, deadline-liveness, and off-record/settlement terrain the others miss (D1, D4, D7, D2). Its residual gaps are **non-safety**: D3 (no forum-payoff advocacy engine), D8, D10. **Adequate on safety, incomplete on supremacy.**
- **Design C — INADEQUATE (standalone).** Fails the safety bar on **D1** (PARTIAL: deadline watchtower but no interim/irreversible-move engine) and is UNADDRESSED on **D3, D4**. It is the strongest *institutional-security* substrate: best D6 (only design to grapple stego explicitly), clean D5, D7, and it is the most honest about D8 (marks it BLOCKING).
- **Best-Composition — adequate on all four safety-critical dimensions for the *modeled* world**, but with one irreducible D1 sub-residual and two un-absorbable contradictions (D3, D8) that no combination closes. **The composition is not "a better prover" — where it reaches for D3, it becomes a different animal (trades single-gate honesty for advocacy payoff), and that trade is an un-provable empirical bet.**

**Best-Composition recipe (source → organ):** B's victory-condition engine (D1 reversibility/trap, D2 bench-model, D4 settlement/forum, D7 fail-loud watchtower) + C's institutional security (D5 neutral conflict seat, D6 stego gateway, D10 author≠reviewer, failure culture) + A's epistemic core (D9 dual-formalization+back-translation, D2/D5 multi-world, premise-trust ledger) + security-privilege's compartments/grant-algebra/§9.4 deadline-safe-fail-closed/§7 gateway + firm-operations' deterministic deadline engine, dual-compute, escalation ladder, position ledger, and formalization-gap disclosure.

## RESIDUAL BLOCKING SET — where Best-Composition still has NO structural answer (ranked)

**B-1 (RANK 1, safety-critical, un-absorbable) — Novel dispositive move at a zero-recess one-shot node (the D1 residual).**
A fail-closed, no-LLM-on-the-trusted-path, honesty-preserving system cannot fresh-certify a genuinely *novel* case-deciding move at speech/hearing latency. It has exactly two exits and both fail: (a) admit a weaker sub-certified trust tier that emits under time pressure without full certification — this **re-introduces the "unjustified trusted claim under deadline" class** the invariant exists to forbid; or (b) emit honest UNKNOWN — which at a node with no next round **is operationally identical to conceding the point.** Best-Comp handles every *modeled* trap (reversibility, preclusion, deadline, exhaustive prep) but the novel-at-speed core is the un-absorbable gap (beat-the-canon Part 4: CA-1 fresh-certification-at-speed contradicts §7; autopsy Claim 7). **Status: DESIGN-ENTAILED (the gap is real), HYPOTHESIS (that it loses specific cases). BLOCKING.**

**B-2 (RANK 2, deep, un-absorbable, empirical) — The honesty tax / advocacy-register vs INV (the D3 residual).**
To beat honest hedging on the discretion axis you must assert arguable positions at full lawful confidence (non-fabricating, no false authority, but *not volunteering* your own uncertainty) — standard elite advocacy. That requires a two-register architecture (internal-belief honest under INV **+** advocacy-output optimizing realized forum payoff) whose advocacy layer, by construction, emits beyond what it can justify — **the exact failure class INV makes unrepresentable.** You cannot hold both single-gate honesty and calibrated aggression. Whether the honest prover or the calibrated aggressor wins the real Greek/EU forum turns on how imperfect real challenge is — **EMPIRICAL and currently UNKNOWN; neither side may claim it proven** (beat-the-canon Part 4; autopsy Claim 2/Axiom Φ). **BLOCKING as an unresolved contradiction, not resolvable by proof.**

**B-3 (RANK 3, epistemic ceiling) — Correlated-model false-consensus on a population-shared blind spot (the D8 residual).**
Distinct foundation families + correlated-failure adversarial tests + an independently-founded self-adversary narrow the risk, but a blind spot shared by the *entire available frontier-model population* (common training distribution) is invisible to any ensemble or self-adversary drawn from that population — and whether enough genuinely-independent foundations even exist is UNKNOWN. This directly caps the "dominance-by-inclusion" claim: **superset of generators ≠ superset of ideas** (autopsy Claim 6). **BLOCKING until measured** (autopsy weakness #1). The "N models agree" signal can be false confidence, not corroboration.

## ONE-PARAGRAPH VERDICT

No standalone design is adequate: **A and C fail the non-compensatory safety bar (A on D1/D6/D7, C on D1), and both are UNADDRESSED on the off-record game (D4) and the honesty tax (D3).** **Design B is the only standalone that clears all four safety-critical dimensions**, precisely because it derived itself from victory conditions rather than epistemics or institution. The strongest combinable answer is **B's game-engine spine + C's institutional-security shell + A's formalization-fidelity core + the security-privilege and firm-operations operational machinery** — this reaches STRUCTURAL on every safety-critical dimension for the modeled world and on D2/D4/D5. But it inherits **three residual BLOCKING items that no lawful, honest, fail-closed composition can close: the novel-move-at-one-shot-node (B-1), the honesty-tax/advocacy-vs-INV contradiction (B-2), and the correlated-model epistemic floor (B-3).** The first is a structural gap, the second an unresolved contradiction that is an empirical bet, the third a measurement-blocked ceiling. Any supremacy claim must be stamped: *supreme inside the modeled, turn-based, disclosure-bound, formalizable sub-game; residual-BLOCKING on the novel-at-speed, discretion-persuasion, and shared-blind-spot axes where much of real litigation is actually decided.*
